#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Merc
Source: Dark Matter Revised
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Blaster Melee.*** When you attack with a blaster, you can replace its mastery property with the Bludgeon property for that attack. You can use the Bludgeon mastery property of the blaster, even if you don’t have the Weapon Mastery feature, and it doesn’t count against the number of weapons you can use.

***Point Blank.*** When you hit a creature within 10 feet of you with a ranged attack roll using a weapon that has the Heavy or Two-Handed properties, the target takes extra damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus. This damage is the same type dealt by the weapon.

***Tactical Signals.*** You learn [[Languages\|Common Sign Language]]. You can communicate tactical messages of 10 words or fewer with a creature that can see the message. The creature doesn’t need to share a language with you to understand this message, but it must be able to understand at least one language.
