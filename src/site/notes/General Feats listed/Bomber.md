#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Bomber
Source: Astarion's Book of Hungers
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Far Lobber.*** When you use the Attack action to throw a bomb, vial or flask, you can target an object or creature you can see within 40 feet of yourself.

***Long Shots.*** Attacking at long range doesn’t impose Disadvantage on your attack rolls with Thrown weapons.
