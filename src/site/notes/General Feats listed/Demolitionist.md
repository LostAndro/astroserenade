#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Demolitionist
Source: Dark Matter Revised
*General Feat (Prerequisites: Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Grenade.*** When you finish a Long Rest, you can make one [[Outrunning Gear\|Concussion Grenade]], [[Outrunning Gear\|Flashbang]], or [[Outrunning Gear\|Freeze Bomb]]. This item lasts until you finish another Long Rest, at which point it harmlessly falls apart.

***Improved Ordinance.*** Your Bombs, [[Outrunning Gear\|Bottled Lightning]], [[Outrunning Gear\|Concussion Grenades]], [[Outrunning Gear\|Flashbangs]], and [[Outrunning Gear\|Freeze Bombs]] use the following DC if it is higher: 8 plus the modifier of the ability increased by this feat and your Proficiency Bonus.
