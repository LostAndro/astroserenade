#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Mirage Maestro
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You have developed an exceptional capacity for discerning and producing illusions. You gain the following benefits.

***Deft Illusionist.*** You learn the [[Minor Illusion\|Minor Illusion]] cantrip. You can produce two separate illusions with a single casting of the spell. The spell's spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.

***Illusion Acuity.*** You can take the Study action as a Bonus Action when attempting to examine illusions, and you have Advantage on any D20 Test to do so.
