#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Elemental Familiar
Source: Arcana Unleashed
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, [[Origin Feats listed/Familiar Friend\|Familiar Friend]] Feat)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Elemental Energy.*** You learn how to imbue your familiar with elemental power. When you cast the [[Find Familiar\|Find Familiar]] spell, choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder damage. Your familiar is imbued with this energy until you cast [[Find Familiar\|Find Familiar]] again, granting it the follow benefits:

**Elemental Resistance.** Your familiar has Resistance to the chosen damage type.
**Energy Pulse.** As a Bonus Action, you command your familiar to unleash a bust of elemental energy. Your familiar must be within 120 feet of you and take a Reaction to unleash this burst. Each creature in a 5-foot Emanation originating from your familiar makes a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 plus your spellcasting ability modifier for the [[Find Familiar\|Find Familiar]] spell and your Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, a creature takes 2d4 damage of the chosen type, and if the creature is Medium or smaller, it has the Prone condition.
