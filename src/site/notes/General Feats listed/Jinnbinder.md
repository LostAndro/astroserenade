#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Jinnbinder
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

A minor jinn is bound to your soul, willing to serve your whims. You gain the following benefits.

***Imaginal Attendant.*** You can cast the [[Unseen Servant\|Unseen Servant]] spell without expending a spell slot. The servant disappears if you cast this spell again with this feat. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

***Minor Wish.*** You can take a Bonus Action to speak your desire, which is granted with a small fraction of a typical wish’s power. When you make this wish, choose one of the boons below. All effects, items, and spells created by the wish last for 8 hours.
Once you take this Bonus Action, you can’t do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

| 1d4 | Minor Wish Boon                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Health.** Roll a number of d6s equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and add them together. You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to the result.                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 2   | **Wealth.** You create one object or up to ten pieces of ammunition of a combined value no higher than 25 × your Proficiency Bonus. Your creation is nonmagical and appears in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you. The item can be a weapon, a Shield, a set of Artisan’s Tools, a gemstone, or an art object. |
| 3   | **Love.** You cast the [[Charm Person\|Charm Person]] spell without a spell slot or spell components. The target doesn’t know it was Charmed by you when the spell ends. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell.                                                                                                     |
| 4   | **Knowledge.** You gain proficiency in one skill or learn one language of your choice.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
