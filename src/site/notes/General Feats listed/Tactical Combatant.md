#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Tactical Combatant
Source: DND Beyond Drops - July 2026
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Buffering Strike.*** When you hit a creature with a weapon, you can gain Temporary Hit Points equal to the total number rolled on the weapon’s damage dice. Once you use this benefit, you can’t use it again until you roll Initiative or finish a Short or Long Rest.

***Honed Instincts.*** When you fail an ability check, you can roll 1d6 and add the number rolled to the ability check, potentially turning it into a success. Once you use this benefit, you can’t use it again until you roll Initiative or finish a Short or Long Rest.
