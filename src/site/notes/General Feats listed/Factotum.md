#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Factotum
Source: Dark Matter Revised
*General Feat (Prerequisites: Level 4+)*

You one of the following options of your choice. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can replace the chosen option with a different one. You can use the chosen option a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

**Flurry of Blows.** You can make two Unarmed Strikes as a Bonus Action.

**Reckless Attack.** When you make your first attack roll on your turn, you can decide to attack recklessly. Doing so gives you Advantage on attack rolls using Strength until the start of your next turn, but attack rolls against you have Advantage during that time.

**Second Wind.** As a Bonus Action, you can regain Hit Points equal to 1d10 plus half your character level.

**Sneak Attack.** Once per turn, you can deal an extra 1d6 damage to one creature you hit with an attack roll if you have Advantage on the roll and the attack uses a Finesse or a Ranged weapon. The extra damage's type is the same as the weapon's type. You don't need Advantage on the attack roll if at least one of your allies is within 5 feet of the target, the ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition, and you don't have Disadvantage on the attack roll. The extra damage increases by 1d6 when you reach character level 7 (2d6), 11 (3d6), 15 (4d6), and 19 (5d6).
