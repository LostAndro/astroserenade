# Genie Magic
Source: Forgotten Realms - Heroes of Faerun
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Wish Magic.*** As a Magic action, you can cast a level 1 spell of your choice from the Esper spell list that has a casting time of an action. Once you use this benefit, you can’t do so again until you finish a Long Rest. The spell’s spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.
When you reach level 11, the spell you cast with this feat is cast as though using a level 2 spell slot. When you reach level 17, the spell is cast as though using a level 3 spell slot.
