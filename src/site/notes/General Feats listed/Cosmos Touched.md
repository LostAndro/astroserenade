#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Cosmos Touched
Source: Steinhardt's Guide To The Eldritch Hunt
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Cosmos Magic.*** Choose one level 1 spell from the Abjuration or Transmutation school of magic. You always have that spell and the [[Alter Self\|Alter Self]] spell prepared. You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot and you must finish a Long Rest before you can cast each spell in this way again. You can also cast either spell using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. Your spellcasting ability for the spells is the ability increased by this feat.
