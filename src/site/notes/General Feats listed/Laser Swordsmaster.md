#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Laser Swordsmaster
Source: Dark Matter Revised
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Cauterizing Critical.*** When you score a Critical Hit with an Antimatter Dagger, Laser Claws, Laser Sword, or Laser Greatsword, you can rake the target with the blade. Instead of rolling the attack’s damage dice twice, you add the attack’s maximum damage to the roll.

***Deflect Blaster.*** If you’re holding an Antimatter Dagger, Laser Claws, Laser Sword, or Laser Greatsword, and another creature hits you with a ranged attack using a blaster, you can take a Reaction to add your Proficiency Bonus to your Armor Class for this attack. If this causes the attack to miss, you can deflect the blaster bolt toward a creature you can see within 60 feet of yourself.
The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 plus the ability modifier of the score increased by this feat and your Proficiency Bonus) or take damage as if hit by the triggering attack.
