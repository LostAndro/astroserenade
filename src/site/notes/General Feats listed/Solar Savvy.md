#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Solar Savvy
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

Your eyes have adjusted to long days in the desert sun, turning its blindingly bright light into an advantage rather than a setback. You gain the following benefits.

***Unblinking.*** You have Advantage on saving throws to end or avoid being Blinded by spells or effects that produce light.

***Sunlit Clarity.*** While in sunlight, you have Advantage on Investigation and Perception checks. Additionally, while in sunlight, visual illusions appear transparent to you, and you automatically succeed on saving throws against them.
