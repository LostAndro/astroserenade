#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Desert Willpower
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

Whether by sheer fortitude or unflinching willpower, you’ve learned to ignore the wear and tear on your body, postponing the pain until you can sleep it off. You gain the following benefits.

***Tireless Traveller.*** While you have 3 or fewer Exhaustion levels, you don't suffer the effects of the Exhaustion condition. You are still considered to have Exhaustion levels, and suffer any additional effects associated with them (such as those of diseases). If you have 4 or more Exhaustion levels, you suffer the effects of the condition as normal.
