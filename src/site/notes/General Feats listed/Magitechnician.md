#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Magitechnician
Source: Dark Matter Revised
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Magic Item Expert.*** If one of your magic items calls for a saving throw, it uses the following DC if it is higher: 8 plus the modifier of the ability increased by this feat and your Proficiency Bonus.

***Magic Item Recharge.*** When you finish a Short Rest, you can recharge a magic item that has charges or properties that recharge at the next dawn. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
