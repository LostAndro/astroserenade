#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Mithridatist
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

Through regularly consuming small doses of harmful foodstuffs, you developed a resilience against their harmful effects. You gain the following benefits.

***Acquired Taste.*** You have Advantage on Constitution saving throws you make to end or avoid being Poisoned, as well as ability checks made to detect and identify poisons.

***Practised Fortitude.*** Whenever you take Poison damage, you build a temporary tolerance to those toxins. Until you finish a Long Rest, any subsequent Poison damage from the same type of creature or poison is reduced by an amount equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum reduction of 1).
