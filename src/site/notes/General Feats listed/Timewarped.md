#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Timewarped
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

Time travel leaves its mark on more than just the monsters and world around you, and you’ve learned how to channel the power of paradoxes in useful ways. You gain the following benefits.

***Ageless.*** You can't be aged magically, and you cease visibly aging.

***Initiative Swap.*** Immediately after you roll Initiative, you can take a Reaction to swap your Initiative count with the Initiative count of one ally within 30 feet of you in the same combat. You can’t make this swap if you or the ally has the Incapacitated condition.
