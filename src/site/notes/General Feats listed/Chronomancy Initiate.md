#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Chronomancy Initiate
Source: Dark Matter Revised
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Spellcasting or Occult Magic Feature)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Cantrip***. You learn the Moment to Think cantrip.

***Expanded Spell List.*** Spells with the [Chronomancy] tag are added to your spell list. The spells [[Haste\|Haste]], [[Slow\|Slow]], and [[Time Stop\|Time Stop]] have the [Chronomancy] tag.

***Prepared Spells.*** Choose a number of spells with the [Chronomancy] tag equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You always have these spells prepared. Whenever you gain a new level, you can replace one of these spells with a different spell with the [Chronomancy] tag.
