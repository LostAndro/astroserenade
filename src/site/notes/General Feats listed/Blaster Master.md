#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Blaster Master
Source: Dark Matter Revised
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Dexterity 13+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Blaster Roulette.*** If you roll the same number of two damage dice for a blaster, you deal bonus damage equal to that number. You can only add this bonus damage once per turn.

***Ignore Cooldown.*** You ignore the Cooldown property of weapons.

***Run and Gun.*** When you take the Dash or Disengage action, you can make a ranged attack using a blaster as a Bonus Action.
