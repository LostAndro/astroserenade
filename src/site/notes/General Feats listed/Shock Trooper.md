#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Shock Trooper
Source: Dark Matter Revised
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Strength 13+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***First Strike.*** You have Advantage attack rolls you make with Melee weapons and Unarmed Strikes during the first round of combat.

***Rapid Advance.*** On the first round of combat, your Speed is doubled and Difficult Terrain doesn’t cost you extra movement.
