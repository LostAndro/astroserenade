# Arcane Geometry
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Spellcasting or Occult Magic Feature)*

Spells come in many shapes and sizes, but most burst forth in regular geometric forms— cones, cubes, lines, spheres. With a greater awareness of these patterns and exercises in control, you’ve learned to twist and sculpt your magic to better suit your needs. You gain the following benefits.

***Precision Casting.*** Whenever you cast a level 1+ spell that creates a Cone, Cube, Line, or Sphere, you can transform the affected area into a different shape using the Arcane Geometry Shapes table. The location of the area's point of origin remains unchanged.

## Arcane Geometry Shape

| Starting Shape | Cone                                                           | Cube                                                               | Line                                                                       | Sphere                                                           |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cone           | —                                                              | Cube with the length of each side equal to the Cone’s length ÷ 2   | 5-foot-wide Line with a length equal to the Cone’s length × 3              | Sphere with a radius equal to the Cone’s length ÷ 2              |
| Cube           | Cone with a length equal to the length of the Cube’s sides × 2 | —                                                                  | 5-foot-wide Line with a length equal to the length of the Cube’s sides × 4 | Sphere with a radius equal to the length of the Cube’s sides ÷ 2 |
| Line           | Cone with a length equal to the Line’s length ÷ 2              | Cube with the length of each side equal to the Line’s length ÷ 4   | —                                                                          | Sphere with a radius equal to the Line’s length ÷ 4              |
| Sphere         | Cone with a length equal to the Sphere’s radius × 2.5          | Cube with the length of each side equal to the Sphere’s radius × 2 | 5-foot-wide Line with a length equal to the Sphere’s radius × 5            | —                                                                |
