#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Precognitive
Source: Dark Matter Revised
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Wisdom 13+)*

You feel impressions of events an instant before they happen. When you start your turn, you can roll a d20 and record the number rolled. You can replace any D20 Test made by you or a creature that you can see before the start of your next turn with this roll.
You can use this feat a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
