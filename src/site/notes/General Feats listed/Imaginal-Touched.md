#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Imaginal-Touched
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

Time spent connected to the Imaginal Plane has expanded the powers of your mind and turned your thoughts into magic. You gain the following benefits.

***Imaginal Magic.*** Choose one level 1 spell from the Conjuration or Transmutation school of magic. You always have that spell and the [[Hallucinogenic Cloud\|Hallucinogenic Cloud]] spell prepared. You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot. Once you cast either spell in this way, you can't cast that spell in this way again until you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spells' spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.
