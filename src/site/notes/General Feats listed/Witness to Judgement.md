#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Witness to Judgement
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Have Died)*

You died, but escaped the eyes of the gods of judgement before they could assign you an afterlife. Your soul, marked by what it has seen, fights to hide its sinful nature. You gain the following benefits.

***Afterlife Inoculation.*** You have Resistance to Necrotic or Radiant damage (your choice when you gain this feat).

***Flee Fate.*** You can cast the Arcanist's Magic Aura spell without expending a spell slot, but can target only yourself when you do so. The spell's spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.

***Kindred Spirits.*** You can cast the [[Speak With Dead\|Speak With Dead]] spell once without expending a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. When you cast the spell in this way, the corpse's animating spirit recognizes you as a peer and doesn't lie to you. The spell's spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.
