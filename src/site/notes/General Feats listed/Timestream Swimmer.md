#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Timestream Swimmer
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You swim up and down the river of time, embodying different versions of yourself to suit your whims. You gain the following benefits.

***Alternate Evolution.*** You always have the Alter Self spell prepared. You can cast it once without expending a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

***More Than Just a Number.*** As a Bonus Action, you can become a younger or older version of your adult self, granting the corresponding benefits below for 10 minutes. Once you alter your age with this feat, you can’t do so again until you finish a Long Rest. The versions are listed here:

**Younger Self.** The vigour of youth flows through you. You have Advantage on ability checks and saving throws that use Strength or Constitution.

**Older Self.** Good sense and experience follow your decisions. You have Advantage on ability checks and saving throws that use Intelligence or Wisdom.
