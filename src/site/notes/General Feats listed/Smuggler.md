#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Smuggler
Source: Dark Matter Revised
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Charisma 13+)*

You gain the following benefits.

***Inconspicuous Cargo.*** You have Advantage on ability checks you make to hide, move, sell, and lie about cargo.

***Shoot First.*** When you roll Initiative and don’t have Disadvantage, you can draw a blaster and make a ranged attack using it.
