#### [[General Feats\|General Feats]]
# Borrowed Future
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Spellcasting or Occult Magic Feature)*

You have learned to sacrifice tomorrow for a better today. You gain the following benefits.

***Need it Now.*** When you cast a level 1–5 spell, you can choose to cast it without expending a spell slot. The next time you would regain an expended spell slot of that level, you do not regain it. Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest, and you can’t use it on a spell slot of the same level until you finish a Long Rest.
