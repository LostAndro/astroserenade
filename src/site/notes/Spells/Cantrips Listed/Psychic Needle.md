# Psychic Needle
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Enchantment Cantrip (Cryptist, Esper, Gridrunner)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** 1 round

You drive a point of psychic pain into the mind of a creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on an Intelligence saving throw or take 1d6 Psychic damage and the next saving throw it makes before the end of your next turn has its DC increased by 1d4.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
