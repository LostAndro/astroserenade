# Culling
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Necromancy Cantrip (Cryptist, Gridrunner, Oracle)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet 
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Swirling negative energy surrounds one creature you can see within range and weakens its life force. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or take 1d10 Necrotic damage. If the target is Bloodied, it has Disadvantage on the save.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by 1d10 when you reach levels 5 (2d10), 11 (3d10), and 17 (4d10).
