# Deflection
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Abjuration Cantrip (Cryptist, Esper, Gridrunner, Icon)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Whenever a creature makes an attack roll against you before the spell ends, roll 1d4 and add it to your AC against that attack.
