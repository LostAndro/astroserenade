# Disruption
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Transmutation Cantrip (Gridrunner, Machinist)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You target a creature or nonmagical object and rip it apart on the molecular level. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Force damage, and its AC is reduced by 1 against the next attack roll made against it before the end of your next turn.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
