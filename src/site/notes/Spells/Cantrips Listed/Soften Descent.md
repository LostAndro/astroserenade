# Soften Descent
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
_Evocation Cantrip (Cryptist, Esper, Gridrunner, Machinist, Primalist)_

**Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take when you are about to hit a surface after falling
**Range:** Self
**Components:** S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

With a gesture, you cause an updraft in the air, slowing your descent. You ignore the first 30 feet when calculating damage from the fall.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** This spell ignores an additional 30 feet of falling damage when you reach levels 5 (60 feet), 11 (90 feet), and 17 (120 feet).
