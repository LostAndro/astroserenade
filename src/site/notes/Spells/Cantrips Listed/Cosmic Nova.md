# Cosmic Nova
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Evocation Cantrip (Oracle)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet 
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You speak a word of power and energy bursts from you in a 5-foot Emanation. Each creature in the area must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 1d4 Force damage and be pushed 5 feet straight away from you.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage and distance increases by 1d4 and 5 feet when you reach levels 5 (2d4 and 10 feet), 11 (3d4 and 15 feet), and 17 (4d4 and 20 feet).
