# Eldritch Orb
Source: Dark Matter Revised
_Evocation Cantrip (Cryptist)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You fling a ball of eldritch energy at a creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Force damage and explodes in a 5-foot Emanation. Each creature in the Emanation makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking half the amount of Force damage on a failed save, or none on a successful one.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
