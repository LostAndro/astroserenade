# Eye of Ra
Source: Dark Matter Revised
_Evocation Cantrip (Oracle)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** M (a Holy Symbol)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A beam of sunlight streaks from each of your eyes. Make two spell attack rolls directed at one target or two different targets within range. On a hit, the target takes 1d4 Radiant damage and the next attack roll against it with a weapon or Unarmed Strike before the start of your next turn has a 1d4 bonus. This bonus is 1d6 if you hit the target with both spell attack rolls.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage of each beam increases by 1d4 when you reach levels 5 (2d4), 11 (3d4), and 17 (4d4).
