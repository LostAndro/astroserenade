# Preserve
Source: Grimhollow: Transformed
_Transmutation Cantrip (Cryptist, Esper, Gridrunner, Icon, Machinist, Oracle)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a sack containing a pinch of salt)
**Duration:** 12 hours

Food and other perishable items weighing 5 pounds or less that you place in a sack don’t age or decay for the duration.
