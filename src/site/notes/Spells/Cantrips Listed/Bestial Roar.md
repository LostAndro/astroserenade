# Bestial Roar
Source: Steinhardt's Guide To The Eldritch
_Evocation Cantrip (Esper, Gridrunner, Machinist)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You release a loud, monstrous roar mimicking those of ravenous beasts, causing shockwaves around you. Each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from you is pushed 10 feet away from you and must succeed on a Strength saving throw or have the Prone condition.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The size of the Emanation increases by 5 feet when you reach levels 5 (15 feet), 11 (20 feet), and 17 (25 feet).
