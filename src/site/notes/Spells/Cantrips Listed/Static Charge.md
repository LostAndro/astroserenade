# Static Charge
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
_Abjuration Cantrip (Esper, Gridrunner, Machinist)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** S, M (a pair of socks)  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a strong static charge in a creature you touch. The next time the target is hit with a melee weapon attack before the start of your next turn, the attacker takes 1d6 Lightning damage.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by 1d6 when you reach levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
