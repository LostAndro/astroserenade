# Debug
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Transmutation Cantrip (Gridrunner, Machinist)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature or an electronic device and create one of the following effects

**Diagnostic.** You learn what conditions and other nonmagical harmful effects are affecting the creature you are touching.

**Enhance.** You cause the device to magnify and improve the quality of one image or video frame stored on or displayed by a device. Ability checks made for the Search or Study actions using the image before the start of your next turn have Advantage.

**Repair Software.** You repair a minor error, corruption, or fault in the device’s software, such as a damaged file or broken link. The DC of ability checks to use the device or program is reduced by 1 for 1 minute. You can’t use this effect on the same target again until you finish a Long Rest.
