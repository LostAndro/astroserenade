# Earthen Fist
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
_Conjuration Cantrip (Primalist)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 10 feet
**Components:** S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You conjure a fist or foot made of rock and earth, punching it towards a creature within 10 feet of you. Make a melee spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Bludgeoning damage and must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, you either knock it Prone or push it 5 feet directly away from you (your choice).

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage and distance a target is pushed increases when you reach levels 5 (2d8 and 10 feet), 11 (3d8 and 15 feet), and 17 (4d8 and 20 feet).
