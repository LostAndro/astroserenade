# Dimness
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Evocation Cantrip (Cryptist, Esper, Gridrunner, Oracle)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** S
**Duration:** 1 minute

You shed dimness in a 40-foot Emanation for the duration. In this area, Bright Light and Darkness become Dim Light.
