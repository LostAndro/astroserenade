# Gravity Whip
Source: Steinhardt's Guide To The Eldritch
_Evocation Cantrip (Cryptist, Esper, Gridrunner, Machinist)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 10 feet  
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You weave gravity in your palm before striking. Make a melee spell attack against a creature within 10 feet of you. On a hit, the target takes 1d4 Bludgeoning damage and is pushed 5 feet in a straight line in a direction of your choice. In addition, its Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by 1d4 and the distance a creature is pushed increases by 5 feet when you reach levels 5 (2d4; 10 feet), 11 (3d4; 15 feet), 17 (4d4; 20 feet)
