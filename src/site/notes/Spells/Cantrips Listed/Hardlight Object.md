# Hardlight Object
Source: Dark Matter Revised
_Conjuration Cantrip (Esper, Gridrunner, Machinist)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (an emerald ring worth 30+ GN)
**Duration:** 1 hour

You compress light into a facsimile of one of the following objects: Bell, Chain, Crowbar, Flask, Grappling Hook, Iron Spikes (10), Rope, Sack, Signal Whistle, or a Shovel. An object created by this spell sheds Dim Light in a 5-foot radius and lasts for the duration. If you cast this spell multiple times, you can have up to three hardlight objects at a time. You can dismiss an object on your turn (no action required).
