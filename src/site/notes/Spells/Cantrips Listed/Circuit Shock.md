# Circuit Shock
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Evocation Cantrip (Esper, Gridrunner, Machinist)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet 
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a temporary circuit with a creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Lightning damage. If you roll an 8 on a damage die for this spell, the shock jumps to another target you can see within 15 feet of the first. Make a new attack roll against the second target. If you hit, this new damage roll can’t cause another jump.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
