# Technomancy
Source: Dark Matter Revised
_Transmutation Cantrip (Esper, Gridrunner, Machinist)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

With a complex input gesture, you can manipulate a technological device you can see within range. You can use this ability to perform any basic operation on the device, including, but not limited to: pressing an external button, entering a dozen keystrokes of data, performing an authorized operation (such as opening an unlocked door), dimming or brightening a screen, or muting a device that makes noise. You can’t disconnect or reconnect wires, or perform an unauthorized operation without making a Hacking attempt.
