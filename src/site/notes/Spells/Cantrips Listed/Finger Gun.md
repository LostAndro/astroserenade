# Finger Gun
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
_Evocation Cantrip (Cryptist, Esper, Primalist)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 120 feet
**Components:** S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You point a finger at a target and release a bullet of arcane energy from it. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d6 Force damage. You score a Critical Hit with this spell if you roll a 19 or higher on the d20.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by 1d6 and the minimum roll you need on the d20 to score a Critical Hit decreases by 1 when you reach levels 5 (2d6 and 18), 11 (3d6 and 17), and 17 (4d6 and 16).
