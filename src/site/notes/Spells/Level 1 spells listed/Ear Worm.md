# Ear Worm
Source: Dark Matter Revised
_Level 1 Enchantment (Icon)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You hum a catchy tune to a creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or lose Concentration on any spell or effect and have the Deafened condition until the spell ends. For the duration, the Deafened target can’t maintain Concentration on any spell or effect.
