# Life Cycle
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Level 1 Necromancy (Oracle, Primalist, Tracer)_

**Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take when a creature you can see within 30 feet of you drops to 0 Hit Points
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a seed)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

One creature of your choice within 10 feet of the triggering creature makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 2d6 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. The triggering creature then regains Hit Points equal to half the damage dealt.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
