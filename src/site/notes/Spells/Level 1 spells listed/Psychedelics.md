# Psychedelics
Source: Dark Matter Revised
_Level 1 Illusion (Icon, Primalist, Tracer)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a mushroom)
**Duration:** 1 round

For a moment, the colors in a 20-foot Emanation originating from you shift in a rainbow swirl. Each creature you choose in the Emanation has Disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes until the spell ends at the end of your next turn.

Additionally, the colors of objects you choose within range change randomly and remain brightly and unnaturally colored for 1 hour.
