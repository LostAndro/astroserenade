# Accelerate Fall
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Level 1 Transmutation (Esper, Gridrunner)_

**Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take when you or a creature you can see within 60 feet of you falls  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 minute

Choose up to five falling creatures within range. A target’s rate of descent doubles until the spell ends. If a target lands before the spell ends, it takes double damage from the fall, and the spell ends for it.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** If you cast this spell using a level 5+ spell slot, its range increases to 20 miles, it can target vehicles in addition to creatures, and the duration increases to 10 minutes.
