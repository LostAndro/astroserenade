# Horrific Demise
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Level 1 Illusion (Cryptist, Esper)_

**Casting Time:** Reaction, which you take when you see a creature reduced to 0 Hit Points within 30 feet of yourself
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 minute

One creature that you can see within range is momentarily horrified by the takedown. It must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the Frightened condition for the duration. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the spell on itself on a success.
