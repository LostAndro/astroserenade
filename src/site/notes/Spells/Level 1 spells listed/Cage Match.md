# Cage Match
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Level 1 Conjuration (Enforcer)_

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Components:** V, M (a bit of chain)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You create an ephemeral fighting cage that only constrains you and one other creature. One creature you can see within range makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the target has Disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you. If it attempts to move to a space more than 30 feet away from you, it takes 4d10 Force damage that ignores Resistance, its Speed is 0 until the start of its next turn, and the spell ends. The cage doesn’t affect any other creature.

The spell also ends if you make an attack roll against a creature other than the target, if you cast a spell on an enemy other than the target, if an ally of yours damages the target, or if you end your turn more than 30 feet away from the target.
