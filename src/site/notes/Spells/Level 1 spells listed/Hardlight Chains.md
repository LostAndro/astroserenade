# Hardlight Chains
Source: Dark Matter Revised
_Level 1 Conjuration (Esper, Gridrunner, Icon, Machinist)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (an emerald ring worth 30+ GN)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Entwining threads of light into shimmering chains, you attempt to bind a creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or have the Restrained condition for the duration. A Restrained creature can take an action to make a Strength (Athletics) check against your spell save DC. On a success, it frees itself from the chains and is no longer Restrained by them.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.
