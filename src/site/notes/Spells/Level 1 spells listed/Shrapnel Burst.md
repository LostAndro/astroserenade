# Shrapnel Burst
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Level 1 Conjuration (Tracer)_

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a target with a Ranged weapon
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, M (a bundle of conductive wiring)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

As you hit the target, an electric shock bursts out of your Ranged weapon or ammunition. The target of the attack and each creature within 5 feet of it make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save a target takes 2d4 Piercing damage, and if it moves from its space before the start of your next turn, it takes an extra 2d4 Piercing damage. On a successful save it takes half as much damage only.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The initial and extra damage each increase by 2d4 for each spell slot level above 1.
