# Failsafe Spellhack
Source: Dark Matter Revised
_Level 1 Abjuration [Spellhack] (Esper, Gridrunner)_

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after you cast a spell with a spell slot that fails to affect any creature
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You recover the spell slot you expended to cast the triggering spell. You can only recover the spell slot of a spell that failed to affect any creature—such as if the spell dealt no damage, all of its spell attack rolls missed, or every creature succeeded its saving throw against the spell and suffered no effect. The GM decides if the triggering spell is applicable.

You can cast this spellhack and another spell with a spell slot on the same turn.
