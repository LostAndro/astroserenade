# Action
Source: Dark Matter Revised
_Level 1 Transmutation [Chronomancy] (Esper, Gridrunner)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a shaving of licorice root)
**Duration:** 1 round

Choose a willing creature that you can see within range. During its next turn, the target gains an additional action. That action can be used only to take the Attack (one weapon attack only), Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Utilize action.
