# Terrifying Smite
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Level 1 Enchantment (Enforcer)_

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a target with a Melee weapon or an Unarmed Strike
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

The target takes an extra 1d8 Psychic damage from the attack, and it has the Frightened condition until the start of your next turn.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 1.
