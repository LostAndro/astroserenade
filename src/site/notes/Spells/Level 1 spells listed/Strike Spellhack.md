# Strike Spellhack
Source: Dark Matter Revised
_Level 1 Evocation [Spellhack] (Enforcer, Esper, Gridrunner, Machinist, Icon, Tracer)_

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take when you cast a spell that requires a saving throw
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a weapon with which you have proficiency and that is worth 1+ GN)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You make an attack with the weapon used in the spell’s casting, channeling the spell through the weapon. The attack uses your spellcasting ability for the attack and damage rolls instead of using Strength or Dexterity. If the attack deals damage, it can be Force damage or the weapon’s normal damage type (your choice).

On a hit, the attack deals damage as normal and also casts the triggering spell targeting the creature hit by the attack. If the spell has an area of effect, you choose how the spell is positioned, but you must include the target in its area. You have Advantage on your saving throw against the spell if you are within its area of effect. The target automatically fails its saving throw against the spell.

On a miss, the spell slot used to cast the triggering spell isn’t expended.

You can cast this spellhack and another spell with a spell slot on the same turn.
