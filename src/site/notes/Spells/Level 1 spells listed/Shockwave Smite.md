# Shockwave Smite
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Level 1 Evocation (Enforcer)_

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a target with a Melee weapon or an Unarmed Strike
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Your strike creates a sonic boom that is audible within 500 feet of you, and the target takes an extra 1d8 Thunder damage from the attack. Additionally, each creature in a 15- foot Cone emanating from you in the direction of the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or have the Prone condition.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 1.
