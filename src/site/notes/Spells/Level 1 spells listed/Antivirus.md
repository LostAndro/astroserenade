# Antivirus
Source: Dark Matter Revised
_Level 1 Abjuration (Gridrunner, Icon, Machinist,  Oracle, Primalist)_

**Casting Time:** Action or Ritual
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You ward a device that you touch from digital interlopers. For the duration, this device can't be disabled by magical effects, such as the [[Spells/Level 1 spells listed/Technical Difficulties\|Technical Difficulties]] or [[Electromagical Pulse\|Electromagical Pulse]] spells, infected by the N-Virus, or inhabited by an AI or similar creature. If the target is inhabited by a creature, that creature is ejected from the device in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of it. Additionally, creatures have Disadvantage on Intelligence (Computers) checks made to hack this device.
