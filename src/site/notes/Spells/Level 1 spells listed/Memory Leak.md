# Memory Leak
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Level 1 Enchantment (Cryptist, Gridrunner)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Junk information clouds the mind of a creature you can see within range. The target makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, it suffers a memory leak for the duration.

While suffering from a memory leak, the target can take one Action, one Bonus Action, one Reaction, or Move on a turn without taking damage. For each subsequent option used on that turn, the target takes 1d6 Psychic damage.

At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
