# Cosmic Favor (Divine Favor)
Source: Player's Handbook
_Level 1 Transmuation (Enforcer)_

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** 1 minute

Until the spell ends, your attacks with weapons deal an extra 1d4 Radiant damage on a hit.
