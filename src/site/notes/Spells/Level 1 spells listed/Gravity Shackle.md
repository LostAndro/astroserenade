# Gravity Shackle
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Level 1 Transmutation (Esper, Gridrunner)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 minute

You bind a creature you can see within range in crushing bands of gravity. The target makes a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 1d10 Force damage, and its speed is halved for the duration. On a successful save, the target takes half damage only, and the spell ends.

For the duration, the target makes a Strength saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a failed save, it takes the Force damage again. On a successful save, the spell ends.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 1.
