# Void Lash
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Level 1 Evocation (Cryptist)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a copper wire)
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You unleash a torrent of lightning at a creature within range, which flows continuously from you to the target. The target’s Speed is halved, and it makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 2d6 Lightning or Necrotic damage (your choice).

On each of your subsequent turns, you can take a Bonus Action to deal 1d6 damage of the chosen type to the target automatically, even if the target succeeded on the saving throw.

The spell ends if the target is ever outside the spell’s range or if it has Total Cover from you.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The initial damage increases by 2d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
