# Armor of Secrets
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Level 1 Abjuration (Esper, Gridrunner)_

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S, M (a shard of black glass)
**Duration:** 1 hour

A shroud of shadowy whispers settles over you. You gain 5 Temporary Hit Points. If a creature hits you with a melee attack roll before the spell ends, the creature takes 5 Psychic damage. The spell ends early if you have no Temporary Hit Points.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The Temporary Hit points and the Psychic damage both increase by 5 for each spell slot level above 1.
