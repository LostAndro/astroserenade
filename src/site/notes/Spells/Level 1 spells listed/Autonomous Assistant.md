# Autonomous Assistant
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Level 1 Conjuration (Gridrunner)_

**Casting Time:** 1 hour or Ritual
**Range:** 10 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (computer chips worth 500+ GN, which the spell consumes)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You gain the service of a special assistant, a digital spirit that appears in an unoccupied space within range and uses the **Autonomous Assistant** stat block. If you already have an assistant from this spell, the assistant is replaced by the new one. You determine the assistant’s appearance, such as a hardlight digital mascot or a robot helper.

**Telepathic Connection.** While your assistant is within 100 feet of you, you can communicate with it telepathically. Additionally, as a Bonus Action, you can see through the assistant’s eyes and hear what it hears until the start of your next turn, gaining the benefits of any special senses it has.

**Combat.** The assistant is an ally to you and your allies. It rolls its own Initiative and acts on its own turn. An assistant can’t attack, but it can take other actions as normal.

**Disappearance of the Assistant.** When the assistant drops to 0 Hit Points, it disappears. It reappears after you cast this spell again. As a Magic action, you can temporarily dismiss the assistant to a pocket dimension. Alternatively, you can dismiss it forever. As a Magic action while it is temporarily dismissed, you can cause it to reappear in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you. Whenever the assistant drops to 0 Hit Points or disappears into the pocket dimension, it leaves behind in its space anything it was wearing or carrying.

**One Assistant Only.** You can’t have more than one assistant at a time.
