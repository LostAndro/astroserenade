# Upgraded Spellhack
Source: Dark Matter Revised
_Level 1 Evocation [Spellhack] (Esper, Gridrunner, Icon, Oracle, Primalist)_

**Casting Time:** Bonus Action, which you take when you cast a spell with a spell slot
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Increase the triggering spell’s effective level by 1, up to a maximum of level 9.

You can cast this spellhack and another spell with a spell slot on the same turn.
