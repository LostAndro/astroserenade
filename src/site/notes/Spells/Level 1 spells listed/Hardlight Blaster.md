# Hardlight Blaster
Source: Dark Matter Revised
_Level 1 Conjuration (Cryptist, Esper, Gridrunner, Icon, Machinist, Tracer)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 10 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (an emerald ring worth 30+ GN)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You wave your hand and conjure a sophisticated blaster made of compressed light, which hovers in the air within 10 feet of yourself and fires a crackling beam of energy at a target you choose within 60 feet of it. Make a ranged spell attack. The blaster is the point of origin for this attack; therefore, the attack can target a creature that isn’t within your line of sight, as long as it is within the blaster’s line of sight. On a hit, the target takes 3d6 Force damage. After this attack, this blaster dissipates.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 1.
