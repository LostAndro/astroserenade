# Void Lash
Source: Neon Odyssey - Outrunner's Handbook
_Level 1 Enchantment (Cryptist)_

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Channeling the Abyssal Void, tentacles of flowing darkness lash out from you. Each creature in a 15-foot Cube originating from you makes a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 3d4 Psychic damage and can’t take Reactions until the start of its next turn. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage only.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d4 for each spell slot level above 1.
