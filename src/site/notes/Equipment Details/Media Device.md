# Media Device
Media devices play audio or visual media files or broadcasts.

***Entertainment Center.*** Entertainment Centers are home entertainment setups with varying sized televisions, along with media players and speakers.

***Portable Audio Player.*** Portable Audio Players are pocket-sized devices meant to play audio files into connected headphones, or on an internal speaker. It has a port to accept a Data Chip and has an equal amount of internal storage.
