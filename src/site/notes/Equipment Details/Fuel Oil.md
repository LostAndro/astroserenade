#### [[Outrunning Gear\|Outrunning Gear]]
# Fuel Oil
You can douse a creature, object, or space with Fuel Oil or use it as fuel, as detailed below. A flask holds 1 pint of Fuel Oil.

***Dousing a Creature or Object.*** When you take the Attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a Fuel Oil flask. Target one creature or object within 20 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 plus your Dexterity modifier and Proficiency Bonus) or be covered in oil. If the target takes Fire damage before the oil dries (after 1 minute), the target takes an extra 5 Fire damage from the burning oil.

***Dousing a Space.*** You can take the Utilize action to pour a Fuel Oil flask on level ground to cover a 5-foot-square area within 5 feet of yourself. If lit, the oil burns until the end of the turn 2 rounds from when the oil was lit (or 12 seconds) and deals 5 Fire damage to any creature that enters the area or ends its turn there. A creature takes this damage only once per turn.

***Fuel.*** Fuel Oil serves as fuel for any fire-based light source or combustion engine device. Once ignited, a flask of Fuel Oil provides 6 hours of burning for wick combustion, 1 hour of operation for a Generator or similar-sized engine, or 8 minutes of operation for a Light Planetary vehicle (roughly 8 miles of travel) or the equivalent.
