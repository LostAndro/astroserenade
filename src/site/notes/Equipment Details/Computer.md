# Computer
Computers serve a variety of functions including displaying entertainment, data storage, playing video games, running and creating programs, and allowing Hypergrid connection. Computers function as Gaming Consoles and execute Simple and Complex Programs. A computer can function as a Spiking Deck, which increases the computer’s price.

***Desktop.*** Desktop computers are larger, more powerful devices that hold large amounts of storage, equivalent to 100 Data Chips. When used to create Complex Programs, a Desktop Computer adds a +5 bonus to the ability check.

***Laptop.*** Laptop Computers are smaller, portable computers. They can store the equivalent of 25 Data Chips.
