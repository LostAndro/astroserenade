#### [[Outrunning Gear\|Outrunning Gear]]
# Repair Parts
Repair Parts are spare components comprised of metal, plastics, and similar materials used to repair vehicles (see “Repairs” in the Vehicles section). After a successful ability check to make the repairs, you expend the parts and restore Hit Points to the vehicle determined by the quality of the parts.
### Repair Parts

| Quality  | HP Regained | Cost     |
| -------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Standard | 2d6 + 2     | 500 GN   |
| Good     | 4d6 + 4     | 1,000 GN |
| Superior | 8d6 + 8     | 2,500 GN |
| Supreme  | 10d6 + 10   | 7,500 GN |
