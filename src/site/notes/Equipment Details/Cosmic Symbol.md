# Cosmic Symbol
A Cosmic Symbol takes on of the forms in the Cosmic Symbol table and is machined or inscribed to channel cosmic magic. An Enforcer or Oracle can use a Cosmic Symbol as a Spellcasting Focus.

The table indicates whether a Cosmic Symbol needs to be held, worn, or born on fabric (such as clothing or a banner) or a suit of armor or a Shield.

### Cosmic Symbols

| Symbol                                   | Weight | Cost   |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------ | ------ |
| Amulet or Badge (worn or held)           | 1 lb.  | 250 GN |
| Insignia (on fabric, armor, or a Shield) | —      | 250 GN |
| Relic (held)                             | 1 lb.  | 250 GN |
