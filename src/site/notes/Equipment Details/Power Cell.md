#### [[Outrunning Gear\|Outrunning Gear]]
# Power Cell
Power Cells are compact energy storage devices compatible with a wide spectrum of equipment. A standard Power Cell can’t be recharged. A Power Cell is empty when first crafted and can be charged to full once by connecting it to a power supply for 24 hours.

***Energy Weapons.*** A Power Cell powers a Ranged weapon that uses it for ammunition or a Melee weapon with the Powered property. A weapon’s Reload property lists how many shots or attacks a cell can power

***Powered Devices.*** The description of a piece of equipment that requires a Power Cell to operate notes how long the cell can power it. You can exchange a Power Cell in a device as an action or a Bonus Action.
