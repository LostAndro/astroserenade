#### [[Outrunning Gear\|Outrunning Gear]]
# Scanner
A Scanner is a handheld device that detects signals, energy, and materials within 200 feet. Using the Scanner as part of the Search or Study action grants Advantage on the roll to detect signals or hazardous conditions, discern the presence or nature of creatures, or the composition of materials.

If you use the Scanner on a creature you can see within 5 feet of you, you have Advantage on ability checks made to detect hidden objects on the creature’s person, or to determine the creature's state of health.
