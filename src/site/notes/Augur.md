#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Augur
Source: Zaman's Guide to the End of Time (And How to Fix It)
*Origin Feat*

You gain the supernatural ability of foretelling, allowing you to glimpse the near future. You gain the following benefits.

***Foresight***. You learn the [[Guidance\|Guidance]] cantrip. Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this feat’s spells (choose when you select this feat).

***Minor Omen***. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you gain a small presage about a moment in the near future. Roll a d4 and record the number rolled. When you or a creature that you can see fails a D20 Test, you can take a Reaction to add the recorded number to the roll.
Each recorded roll can only be used once. When you finish a Long Rest, you lose any unused recorded numbers.

***Repeatable***. You can take this feat more than once. Each time you do so, you learn another cantrip from the Gridrunner or Oracle spell list. In addition, you can roll an additional d4 whenever you finish a Long Rest as part of the Minor Omen benefit, and record the number rolled. You can add the recorded numbers of multiple d4s to a roll as part of the same Reaction.
