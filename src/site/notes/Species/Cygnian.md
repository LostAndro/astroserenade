#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Cygnian
![Cygnian.png](/img/user/images/Cygnian.png)
Cygnians (SIG-nee-in) are the star-touched of [[Agathos\|Agathos]], once mortal beings remade by the will of the gods. Some were heroes who lived by the tenets of their favored deity so completely that they were chosen to ascend and begin their journey anew as a Celestial. Others were selected at birth due to prophecies of greatness in the stars, discovered places or relics of divine import, or caught the eye of a god on a whim.
While some live their new immortal lives in the heavenly realm of [[Agathos\|Agathos]], many return to the galaxy to serve the god that reforged them. 
The physical form of cygnians is comprised of starstuff, with resilient skin and flowy hair that evoke the night sky and statues of sacred stone and metal. They typically share a humanoid visage adorned by star shapes with distinct features that reflect their god, but some retain traits of their original species.
### Cygnian Traits
**Creature Type**: Celestial 
**Size**: Medium (about 5–7 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Cygnian, you have these special traits
	***Ageless***. You don't suffer from or die of old age.
	***Darkvision***. You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.
	***Epic Resolve***. You have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Frightened condition.
	***Glory of Agathos***. You are marked by one of the triads of Agathos: Beacon, Chronicle, Fountain, or Tower (choose the triad when you select this species). You can take a Bonus Action to gain a benefit determined by your triad.
	Once you use this Bonus Action, you can’t use it again until you finish a Long Rest unless you expend Heroic Inspiration to use it again.
	**Beacon**. For 1 minute, when you miss with an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to reroll the d20 and use the new roll.
	**Chronicle**. Choose an ability score. For 1 minute, you can add a bonus equal to your Proficiency Bonus to saving throws using the chosen score.
	**Fountain**. Touch a creature and roll a number of d6s equal to your Proficiency Bonus. The target gains Temporary Hit Points equal to the total rolled, which vanish after 1 minute.
	**Tower**. For 1 minute, when you take damage, you can take a Reaction to reduce the damage taken by 1d12 plus your Proficiency Bonus.
	***Made of Stars***. You have Resistance to Radiant damage. When you take Radiant damage, you can take a Reaction to redirect some of the energy. The next time you deal damage before the end of your next turn, one target takes an extra 1 d6 Radiant damage.
	***Starfall***. When you fall at least 10 feet, you can take a Reaction to either slow or speed your fall until the start of your next turn. If you slow your fall, you fall only 60 feet and take no falling damage if you land during that time.
	If you speed your fall, you descend with comet-like force. You have Resistance to the fall's damage, and one creature within 5 feet of you during the fall takes 2 d6 Radiant damage.
