#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Clawderan
![Clawderan.png](/img/user/images/Clawderan.png)
Clawderan (klaw-DAIR-in) are an energetic species of humanoids with feline traits and are known to take pride in proving themselves in combat and other physical pursuits. Many seek glory to honor [[Myrmona\|Myrmona]] the Paragon, the war goddess with whom they hold a cosmic connection.
While many clawderan seek the galactic battlefronts across the Stardust Rhapsody, some prefer to show off their athletic prowess through sports, dance, high-intensity industrial careers, and other such pursuits
Clawderan builds range from agile to brawny and come in a variety of body and fur types, their feline traits resembling the full spectrum of cats both great and small. They typically have a short layer of fur across their bodies, with the notable exceptions being their manes, tails, and fluffy tufts that might grow on their torso or limbs.
### Clawderan Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 5–7 feet tall) or Small (about 3–4 feet tall), chosen when you select this species
**Speed**: 35 feet

As an Clawderan, you have these special traits
	***Battle Ready***. Once during your turn when you hit with an Unarmed Strike or an attack roll using a weapon with which you have proficiency, you can activate the Push, Slow, or Vex mastery property, in addition to a different mastery property you’re using with that attack.
	***Darkvision***. You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.
	***Eager Advance***. When you roll Initiative, you can move up to your Speed.
	***Paragon’s Blessing***. When you make an attack roll and roll an 18 or 19 on the d20, you can choose to make it a Critical Hit. Once you use this trait, you can’t use it again until you finish a Long Rest.
	***Limber Musculature***. You have proficiency in the Acrobatics or Athletics skill, and you have Advantage on D20 Tests to avoid or end the Grappled or Restrained condition.
