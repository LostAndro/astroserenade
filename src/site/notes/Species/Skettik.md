#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Skettik
![Skettik.png](/img/user/images/Skettik.png)
Skettiks (SKEH-tik) are a flightless avian species suffused with innate necromantic energy, the remnants of a cataclysmic ritual that killed and reanimated the world soul of their homeworld. These warped carrion-eaters use their necrotic magic to craft personal minions or strange relics from bone.
Many skettiks are reclusive, shying away from places touched by garish sunlight, and often find themselves operating in the galaxy’s criminal underworld. Others find great success in business and as merchants, utilizing their shrewdness and skepticism to score the best deals.
Skettiks resemble wingless vultures in a humanoid shape with scaly skin and sparse patches of feathers that range from grayscale to drab shades of other colors. They have long claws on their hands and feet that they use for climbing the huge trees of their homeworld, and their jagged beaks are filled with sharp teeth for cracking bone.
### Skettik Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 5–6 feet tall) 
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Skettik, you have these special traits
	***Bonecraft***. With 1 minute of work, you can magically shape bone into useful forms. You can create a piece of outrunning gear that doesn’t require power and is worth $500 or less, or a Simple weapon that lacks the Ammunition or Powered properties, using an amount of bone equal to the weight of the item. The item crumbles to dust after 24 hours.
	***Carrion Eater***. You can eat spoiled food without issue. Additionally, you have Resistance to Poison damage, and you have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.
	***Canopy Climber***. You have a Climb Speed equal to your Speed.
	***Channeled Necrosis***. When you deal damage, you can change the damage type to Necrotic.
	***Necromantic Curse***. You know the [[Culling\|Culling]] cantrip.
	Additionally, starting at character level 3, you always have the [[Inflict Wounds\|Inflict Wounds]] spell prepared, and at character level 5, you always have the [[Construct Companion\|Construct Companion]] (the Construct is made of bone) spell prepared. You can cast each spell once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast them in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast the spells using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
	Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait (choose ability when you select this species).
	***Rotproof***. You have Resistance to Necrotic damage.
