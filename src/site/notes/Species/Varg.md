# Varg
![Varg.png](/img/user/images/Varg.png)
Vargs (VAHRG) are a vampiric people corrupted by the discordant tone of Entropy through the envious yearning to fill the emptiness within them in the hopes it may quiet their pain. They hunger for the life essence of others, which they drain to briefly satiate themselves. Those who work to control or even master this craving avoid succumbing to its addictive nature. The rest become ravenous killers.
Whether enticed by the ease of feeding within heavily populated areas or brooding in the remote reaches of far-flung worlds, the desire of a varg to fill the pit within themselves often leads them to achieve impressive levels of wealth or power—though it is never enough.
Vargs have pallid skin in desaturated hues, and on their backs are leathery flaps that resemble cloaks. They typically have prominent foreheads, ears that evoke bat wings, and prominent fangs. All vargs have one or more patches of fiendish energy on their chest, neck, or abdomen that serve as windows to the emptiness within.
### Varg Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 4–6 feet tall) or Small (about 2–4 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Varg, you have these special traits
	***Ageless***. You don't suffer from or die of old age.
	***Darkvision***. You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.
	***Drain Energy***. You know the [[Chill Touch\|Chill Touch]] cantrip, and Constitution is your spellcasting ability for it. 
	You can cast the spell as a Bonus Action a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
	***Hollow Flesh***. You have Resistance to Cold damage and Necrotic damage.
	***The Pit Within***. You don’t gain Exhaustion levels from dehydration or malnutrition, but you must drain vital energy to survive. If you go a number of days equal to your Constitution modifier plus 1 (minimum of 1) without damaging a creature with your Chill Touch, you gain 1 Exhaustion level. Until you damage a creature with Chill Touch, you can’t remove Exhaustion levels, and you continue to gain 1 Exhaustion level every 24 hours that pass. This trait can’t cause your Exhaustion level to rise higher than 5.
	If you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points using [[Chill Touch\|Chill Touch]], you remove all Exhaustion levels gained from this trait.
	***Path of Entropy***. When you reach character level 3, you must choose the path your discordant nature takes.
	**Distant Desire**. Your [[Chill Touch\|Chill Touch]] cantrip now has a range of 60 feet. When you damage a creature with it, you gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to twice your Proficiency Bonus.
	**Wings of Night**. Your creature type becomes Fiend, and you grow a pair of bat wings from your shoulders that grant you a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. When you cast [[Chill Touch\|Chill Touch]], you can wrap your wings around the target to gain Advantage on the spell's attack roll.
