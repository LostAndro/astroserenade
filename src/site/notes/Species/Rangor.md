#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Rangor
![Rangor.png](/img/user/images/Rangor.png)
Rangor (RAING-gor) are a hulking, subterranean species with an inherent supernatural force of momentum brought on by the [[aether\|aether]] that suffuses their core. They can propel themselves forward at surprising speeds given their bulk and have the innate ability to become an unstoppable force that can smash through anything in their way.
Having first burrowed out of an asteroid of crystalized aether, rangor typically have a wanderlust that keeps them in outer space. Many of them stay on the move and stop for limited periods of time at space stations, moons, asteroids, and other off-world locations.
Rangor generally have broad, bulky builds with proportionally long arms that end in digging claws and short legs. Their thick hides are mottled with patches of tough scales, and they grow flaps of skin that resemble shaggy hair. A rangor’s nostrils sit in the middle of their forehead, and their short tusks continue to grow as they age.
### Rangor Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 6–8 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Rangor, you have these special traits
	***Burrowing***. You have a Burrow Speed equal to your Speed.
	***Claws***. You have claws you can use to make Unarmed Strikes. When you hit and deal damage with them, you can deal 1d8 Slashing damage plus your Strength modifier instead of the normal damage of an Unarmed Strike, and the target also has the Grappled condition.
	***Darkvision***. You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet.
	***Momentum***. You can take the Dash action as a Bonus Action. When you do so, the next time you deal damage before the end of the turn, the target takes extra Force damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
	***Powerful Build***. You have Advantage on D20 Tests you make to end the Grappled condition. You also count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity.
	***Unstoppable Force***. Starting at character level 3, when you use your Momentum trait, you become an overwhelming focal point of force until the end of the turn. For the duration, the damage die of your claws increases to 1d10, you ignore Difficult Terrain, you deal double damage to objects and structures, and you can move into the space of an object and make one Unarmed Strike against it. If you destroy the object with that attack, you can keep moving, otherwise you return to the space you left and your movement ends.
