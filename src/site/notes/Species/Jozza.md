# Jozza
![Jozza.png](/img/user/images/Jozza.png)
Jozza (JAW-zuh) are a hulking species of humanoids with shark-like features, hailing from a world covered almost entirely by oceans. They have a particular taste for blood and a biological penchant for violence, known best for their large jaws filled with razor-sharp teeth.
Many of them dive into fights with a rowdy glee, and their tough physicality makes them well suited to survive and push through any pain. However, some jozza prefer non-violent battles of wit and word or get their fix through virtual gameplay.
Jozza have coarse, gritty skin that can take on a wide range of colors, although many are shades of gray, blue, brown, and olive. A prominent dorsal fin rises from the top of their head or the back of their neck, and smaller fins grow on each of their arms. They sometimes have serrated exterior plates over their lower jaw or protruding bones at the hinges.
### Jozza Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 6–8 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Jozza, you have these special traits
	***Aquatic***. You can breathe air and water, and you have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.
	***Bloodhunt***. You have Advantage on Wisdom checks to detect or track creatures that are missing any Hit Points.
	***Darkvision***. You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.
	***Frenzied Fury***. You have Advantage on attack rolls against a creature that is Bloodied.
	***Ignore Pain***. When you take damage, you can take a Reaction to expend and roll one Hit Point Die. Reduce the triggering damage by an amount equal to the number rolled plus your Constitution modifier.
	***Violent Delight***. When you deal damage while there is more than one enemy within 10 feet of you, you can deal an extra 1d4 damage. You can deal this extra damage only once per turn.
	The extra damage die increases in size when you reach levels 5 (1d6), 11 (1d8), and 17 (1d10).
