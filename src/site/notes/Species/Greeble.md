#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Greeble
![Greeble.png](/img/user/images/Greeble.png)
Greebles (GREE-bull) are a worm-like species driven by an innate desire to voyage across the galaxy. Said to be blessed by [[Pelogos\|Pelogos]] the Navigator, they possess a supernatural ability to always sense the stars and navigate by their light.
Many greebles are drawn to space travel of all varieties, whether as transport pilots, explorers, or anything in between. This extrasensory power and internal cosmic compass is so strong that none can doubt the navigation skills of the greeble.
Despite their segmented worm biology, Greebles have a humanoid shape with nearly universally thin legs. They have pointed noses, disproportionately large eyes, and soft skin in shades of pink to more earthy tones.
### Greeble Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (4–6 feet tall) or Small (3–4 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Greeble, you have these special traits
	***Cosmic Compass***. You have proficiency with Navigation Tools and in the Survival skill.
	***Darkvision***. You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.
	***Navigator’s Heading***. You always have the [[Augury\|Augury]] spell prepared, and you can cast it once without a spell slot. You regain the ability to cast it that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level. Whenever you cast the spell, if you can see the stars, you require no Material components. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it (choose the ability when you select this species).
	***Segmented Biology***. You have redundant internal organs. When an attack roll against you is a Critical Hit, you can take a Reaction to make it a normal hit instead. Once you use this trait, you can’t use it again until you finish a Long Rest.
	***Squirm***. You have a Burrow Speed equal to your Speed. Additionally, you have Advantage on ability checks and saving throws to avoid or end the Grappled or Restrained conditions. When you fail such a roll, you can take a Reaction to expend and roll a Hit Point Die and add it to the total, possibly turning the failure into a success.
	***Stellar Navigator***. You can see or sense the stars from anywhere in the galaxy, though they are faint during daylight or while an opaque object is between you and the sky. When you succeed on an ability check using Navigator’s Tools or the Survival skill while lost, or to avoid becoming lost, you gain Heroic Inspiration. You can’t gain Heroic Inspiration with this trait again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
