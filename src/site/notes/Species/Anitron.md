#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Anitron
![Anitron.png](/img/user/images/Anitron.png)
Anitrons (AN-ih-tron) are living robots with a hyperspark—a technological soul originating from the [[Hypergrid\|Hypergrid]] and infused with the Harmony’s life energy. Scholars debate whether the first hypersparks manifested spontaneously, or if some inventor gave the first anitron their synthetic spirit.
Their mechanical bodies are each designed for a singular specialty in which they excel and often fixate on executing through free will instead of programmatic dictate.
Constructed from various materials and fashioned into a wide variety of body shapes and sizes, anitrons are one of the most physically diverse species in the galaxy
### Anitron Traits
**Creature Type**: Construct 
**Size**: Medium (about 4–7 feet tall) or Small (about 3–4 feet tall), chosen when you select this species
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Anitron, you have these special traits
	***Anitron Design***. Choose one of the following options. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast using this trait (choose when you select the option).
	**Battle**. You have proficiency in the Athletics or Intimidation skill, and you know the [[Deflection\|Deflection]] cantrip. When you cast the spell, you can modify it so that it doesn’t require Concentration and its duration becomes 1 minute. You can’t modify it in this way again until you finish a Long Rest.
	**Diplomacy**. You have proficiency in the Deception, Insight, or Persuasion skill, and you always have the [[Comprehend Languages\|Comprehend Languages]] spell prepared. You can cast it without a spell slot once, and regain the ability to do so when you finish a Short or Long Rest. You can also cast it using spell slots you have.
	**Technical**. You have proficiency in the Computers, Medicine, or Technology skill, and with one tool of your choice. You also know the [[Mending\|Mending]] cantrip, and its casting time is an action for you.
	***Integrated Tech***. You have a built-in compartment that conceals a weapon, a tool, a Shield, or a piece of outrunning gear of your choice. If the chosen item has a proficiency associated with it, you gain that proficiency. The chosen item must weight 8 lb. or less, and a weapon must have the Light property.
	The item remains hidden until you deploy it as a Bonus Action (DC 20 to detect while stowed). You must have a free hand, and you hold the item or don the Shield as part of the Bonus Action. You stow the item (doffing a Shield) back in its compartment using another Bonus Action.
	**Recharge**. You don’t need sleep, and magic can’t put you to sleep. During a Long Rest, you appear inert but remain conscious. You can finish a Long Rest in 4 hours if you consume a full Power Cell or connect to an energy source.
	**Robotic Resilience**. You have Resistance to Poison damage, and you have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Petrified condition or the Poisoned condition.
	**Technological Being**. You don’t gain Exhaustion levels from dehydration, malnutrition, or suffocation.
