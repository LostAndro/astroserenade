# Zorbrak
![Zorbrak.png](/img/user/Zorbrak.png)
Zorbraks (ZOR-brak) are a bipedal insectile species that have mantis-like features and boast a secondary pair of powerful scythe-like appendages in addition to their arms. They are the consummate hunters with a powerful predator’s instinct and the supernatural ability to stalk their prey.
Zorbraks typically lack any sense of fear, allowing them to seek and kill the most dangerous game across the galaxy without hesitation. Many are instilled with immense pride by the hunting trophies they have won over their lifetime and are driven by the desire to leave a legendary legacy.
Zorbrak body types range from hulking to lithe, and their carapace can come in a wide range of hues from earthy to vibrant. They typically have a protruding, jagged maw and two sensory antennae, and they sometimes grow chitinous tendrils from their head.
### Zorbrak Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 5–7 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Zorbrak, you have these special traits
	***Climb Speed***. You have a Climb Speed equal to your Speed.
	***Darkvision***. You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.
	***Fearless***. You have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Frightened condition.
	***Prey Stalker***. You have proficiency in the Perception, Stealth, or Survival skill.
	***Scything Claws***. You have a second pair of arms that end in sharp, blade-like claws that you can use to make Unarmed Strikes (Damage option only) with the Finesse property. On a hit, your claws deal 1d8 Slashing damage plus your ability modifier instead of the normal damage of an Unarmed Strike.
	Additionally, if you have Advantage on an attack roll using your claws, it scores a Critical Hit on a roll of 19 or 20 on the d20.
	***Wraithform***. As a Bonus Action, you magically gain the Invisible condition until the start of your next turn or until you make a damage roll or force someone to make a saving throw. When the Invisible condition on you from this trait ends, you treat Half Cover as Three-Quarters Cover until the end of your next turn. You can use this Bonus Action a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
