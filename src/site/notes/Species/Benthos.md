# Benthos
![Benthos.png](/img/user/images/Benthos.png)
Benthos (BEN-thoess) are a people forever changed by the eldritch power of the Abyssal Void. Eons ago, their homeworld was ripped into a black hole and lost to the [[Wyrmways\|Wyrmways]], the border dimension between the galaxy and the Void. They irrevocably transformed to survive the alien environment, taking on traits of the Void creatures known as the Deep. In time, they learned to harness dark matter, shaping it to their will and utilizing it in their magic and technology.
Benthos are diminutive people with features reminiscent of deep sea fish and skin in shades of the Void. They typically lack color outside their glowing circular eyes and lamp-like lures at the end of the flexible stalk that grows from their head. Their mouths are especially wide and can unhinge indefinitely
### Benthos Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Small (about 3–4 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Benthos, you have these special traits
	***Born of the Deep***. You have Resistance to Necrotic and Psychic damage.
	***Dark Matter Crafting***. While not in Bright Light, you can take a Magic action to shape shadows into one Small or smaller nonmagical item of your choice worth no more than $1,000. The item must appear in your hand, or in your space on a surface that can support it. The item disappears after a number of hours equal to your Proficiency Bonus or when it is reduced to 0 Hit Points. A creature that examines the object can discern its true nature with a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check. The DC is 8 plus your Proficiency Bonus and the ability modifier you chose for Lure of Shadows.
	Once you create an item with this trait, you can’t do so again until you finish a Long Rest
	***Darkvision***. You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet.
	***Lure of the Deep***. You know the [[Dimness\|Dimness]] cantrip. Additionally, starting at character level 3, you always have the [[Dissonant Whispers\|Dissonant Whispers]] spell prepared, and starting at character level 5, you always have the [[Web\|Web]] spell prepared (the webs are spun shadow). You can cast each spell once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast the spells in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast the spells using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
	You can make a target that fails its saving throw against your [[Dissonant Whispers\|Dissonant Whispers]] move toward you instead of away.
	Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait (choose the ability when you select the species).
	***One with Shadows***. You can take the Hide action while only Lightly Obscured, even if you are within an enemy’s line of sight.
	***Swarming Shoal***. When you deal damage while two or more of your allies are within 10 feet of you, you can deal an extra 1d4 Force damage to one target. You can deal this extra damage only once per turn.
	The damage die changes when you reach character levels 5 (1d6), 11 (1d8), and 17 (1d10).
