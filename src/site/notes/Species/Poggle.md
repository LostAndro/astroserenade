#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Poggle
![Poggle.png](/img/user/images/Poggle.png)
Poggles (PAW-gull) are born lucky. Said to be the creation of the luck goddess [[Tykris\|Tykris]] the Dealer, the favor of fortune touches everything they do. While they use this blessing to seek prosperous and happy lives, they also can share their innate luck with others.
Drawn to games of chance, they can be found at gambling tables across the galaxy. Some use their death-defying fortune to take extremely dangerous jobs where a little luck is required for survival. Others find themselves in the world of finance, amassing their wealth through risky investments.
Poggles are a diminutive species covered in fur that is typically white, brown, gray, or pink, though it can grow in other hues. They have long, sometimes-floppy ears, small, bat-like wings on their back, and a wire-like tail that ends in a colorful puff ball.
### Poggle Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Small (about 2–4 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Poggle, you have these special traits
	***Dealer’s Favor***. You have 4 Luck Dice which are d6s, and you regain 1d4 expended Luck Dice when you finish a Long Rest. When you or a creature you can see within 10 feet of you makes a D20 Test, you can take a Reaction to expend and roll 1 Luck Die and apply it as a bonus or penalty (your choice) to the total.
	The maximum number of Luck Dice you have increases at levels 5 (6 dice), 11 (8 dice), and 17 (10 dice). Additionally, the number of expended Luck Dice you regain increases when you reach levels 5 (1d6), 11 (1d8), and 17 (1d10).
	***Flutter Flight***. Because of your wings, you have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. You can’t use this Fly Speed if you’re wearing Medium or Heavy armor.
	***Go-To Game***. You have proficiency with one Game Set of your choice.
	***Lucky Knack***. You have proficiency in one skill of your choice.
