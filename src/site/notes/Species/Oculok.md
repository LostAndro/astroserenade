#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Oculok
![Oculok.png](/img/user/images/Oculok.png)
Oculoks (AHK-yoo-lahk) are a cyclopean species that hails from a vast underground civilization. They are highly adapted to subterranean life with enhanced monocular vision that can see through inorganic matter and a magnetic touch that can manipulate metal to their needs.
Seeking opportunities to put their metal-bending to use, many oculoks possess a keen interest in building, from structures to vehicles. Their societies typically favor cooperation and rigid structure, and most citizens find purpose in contributing to a collective of any scale.
Oculoks have sturdy bodies covered in scaly, earth-toned skin and protruding armored nodules. Their heads are broad with lateral ridges and a spiked crown, with the most prominent feature being a large, singular eye. Their tails act as a built-in defense mechanism, ending in either a club or spikes.
### Oculok Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 5–7 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Oculok, you have these special traits
	***Cyclopean Focus***. You have proficiency in the Insight, Investigation, or Perception skill.
	***Darkvision***. You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet.
	***Metal Shaper***. You know the [[Mending\|Mending]] cantrip. If you target a metal object, you can change the casting time to a Bonus Action. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it (choose the ability when you select this species).
	Additionally, as a Magic action, you can touch a metal object (or part of a larger metal object or structure) no larger than a 5-foot Cube. The target gains Resistance or Vulnerability to all damage (your choice) for 1 minute. Once you use this action, you can’t use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
	***Stonebreaking Tail***. You have your choice of a spike or club on your tail that you can use to make Unarmed Strikes. When you hit with your tail and deal damage with it, you deal 1d8 Bludgeoning or Piercing damage, as appropriate, plus your Strength modifier instead of the normal damage for an Unarmed Strike.
	***Tunnel Vision***. As a Bonus Action, you can adjust your vision to see through inorganic matter for up to 1 minute while you maintain Concentration on the vision. For the duration, you can see through objects and structures made of stone, metal, plastics, or similar materials to a range of 30 feet beyond the obstruction. The area you view through an object is Lightly Obscured. Living or once-living material of any thickness blocks your sight. You can use this Bonus Action a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
