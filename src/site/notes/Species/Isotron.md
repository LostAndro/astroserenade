#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Isotron
![Isotron.png](/img/user/images/Isotron.png)
Isotrons (EYE-soh-tron) are wholly digital beings brought to life by a synthetic soul called a hyperspark. Originating from the digital realm of the Hypergrid, they are comprised of code that can be compiled into physical forms of hard light. This grants them an innate ability to interface with computers and manipulate software with a touch.
Many isotrons live their entire lives within the [[Hypergrid\|Hypergrid]], but others venture into physical reality to discover new experiences across the Stardust Rhapsody. In such a technological galaxy, isotrons have no trouble finding ways to put their inherent skills to use.
Isotron bodies vary in shape, size, and color, and they are often bedecked with bold or saturated hues that glow from the code within them. Their heads are typically humanoid with built-in visors, but some are entirely flat with a digital face projected upon it.
### Isotron Traits
**Creature Type**: Construct 
**Size**: Medium (about 4–6 feet tall) or Small (about 3–4 feet tall), chosen when you select this species
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Isotron, you have these special traits
	***Data Jump***. You always have the [[Misty Step\|Misty Step]] spell prepared. You can cast it without a spell slot a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
	When you cast the spell using this trait while you are within 5 feet of a power or data connection, if your destination space is also within 5 feet of that connection, you don’t expend a use of this trait.
	Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it (choose the ability when you select this species).
	***Hard Light Body***. You don’t gain Exhaustion levels from dehydration, malnutrition, or suffocation.
	***Hypergrid Soul***. You have Advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects.
	***Native Interfacing***. You have proficiency in the Computers skill, and you can interface with a data port as if you were using a Spiking Deck by touching the port.
	***Surge Protection***. You have Resistance to Lightning damage.
