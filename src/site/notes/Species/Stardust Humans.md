#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Stardust Humans
![Human.png](/img/user/images/Human.png)
Humans are the most populous species of the Stardust Rhapsody by a wide margin, and they can be found on virtually every habitable planet across the galaxy. They have no singular homeworld, and legend says that they simply stepped out of the Harmony on all worlds simultaneously one day.
They are highly adaptable and can make a comfortable life no matter where they’re from or where they choose to settle down. In the face of trials, tribulations, and hardships, the indomitable human spirit oftentimes prevails, allowing them to overcome whatever challenges they face and complete whatever hero’s journey they are set on by fate.
Humans are as diverse in appearance as they are in personality, and come in all shapes, sizes, colors, and physical attributes.
### Stardust Humans Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 4–7 feet tall) or Small (about 3–4 feet tall) chosen when you select this species 
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Stardust Humans, you have these special traits
	***Harmonic Control***. As a Bonus Action, you can roll one Hit Point Die without expending it to regain a number of Hit Points equal to the number rolled plus your Proficiency Bonus. You can use this trait once, and you regain the use of it when you finish a Long Rest.
	***Indomitable Spirit***. When you fail a saving throw, you can choose to succeed on it instead. Once you use this trait, you can’t do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
	***Overcome***. When you roll a 1 or a 20 on a D20 Test, you gain Heroic Inspiration. If the roll has no stakes or consequences when you make it, this trait doesn’t function.
	***Planetary Adaptation***. You gain an [[Origin feat\|Origin feat]] of your choice that has a homeworld prerequisite.
