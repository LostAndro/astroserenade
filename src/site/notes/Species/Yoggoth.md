# Yoggoth
![Yoggoth.png](/img/user/images/Yoggoth.png)
Yoggoths (YAW-goth) are a strange species rumored to have been birthed from the eldritch dreams of the mysterious elder beings of the galaxy. Hailing from a remote homeworld scattered with the ruins of their creators, they experience otherworldly dreams said to be an echo of what brought their people into being.
As the ultimate survivors, yoggoths are extremely resourceful and prone to recycling everything they possibly can. Many are drawn to salvaging operations, but some are driven to uncover the mysteries of their dreams and eldritch origins, not stopping until they discover the answers they seek.
Yoggoths appear like alien flies in a bipedal humanoid shape with both leathery and chitinous hides that are typically earthy in color. They have large glassy eyes, four nostrils on their elongated snouts, and fleshy tendrils that can grow from their head or chin. Two pairs of leathery wings grow from their shoulders, from which they emit their infamous drone.
### Yoggoth Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 4–6 feet tall) or Small (about 3–4 feet tall), chosen when you select this species
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Yoggoth, you have these special traits
	***Buzzing Wings***. You have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. You can’t use this Fly Speed if you’re wearing Medium or Heavy armor.
	***Hypnagogic Droning***. As a Bonus Action, you can create a vibration that resonates with your most alien dreams in a 20-foot Emanation originating from you. The droning lasts for 1 minute or until you lose your Concentration. When you create the Emanation, and at the start of each of your turns for the duration, each creature of your choice in that area must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 plus your Constitution modifier and Proficiency Bonus) or subtract 1d4 from ability checks and attack rolls until the start of your next turn. Once you use this Bonus Action, you can’t use it again until you finish a Long Rest.
	***Lingering Dreams***. When you sleep, your dreams connect to the lingering echoes of the Eldest that created the yoggoth species, gifting you with knowledge you can’t explain. When you finish a Long Rest, you gain proficiency in one skill and with one tool of your choice until you make a different selection with this trait.
	***Sole Survivor***. You have Advantage on saving throws made to avoid or end the Charmed, Exhaustion, or Poisoned conditions.
