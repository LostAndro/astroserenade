# Nematoan
![Nematoan.png](/img/user/images/Nematoan.png)
Nematoans (NEE-muh-TOE-in) are a four-armed amphibian species with a voracious appetite. Their unique stomachs allow them to magically ingest traits of the things that they eat, temporarily enhancing their own abilities.
This has led many nematoans to develop a focus on food and seek out new dishes to taste across the galaxy. Some become chefs or ship’s cooks, while others get into dangerous trades where exotic and even black market delicacies can be procured.
Hailing from a world covered in putrid swamps, nematoans have physical traits that lend themselves to comfortable bog living, including webbed hands and feet, eye stalks, long tongues, and slimy skin that is typically earthy in tone.
### Nematoan Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 4–7 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Nematoan, you have these special traits
	***Amphibious***. You can breathe air and water.
	***Four-Armed***. You have two slightly smaller secondary arms below your primary pair of arms. You can use a secondary arm to manipulate an object, open or close a door or container, pick up or set down a Tiny object, or wield a weapon with the Light property.
	***Gourmand of Variety***. You can eat part of any dead creature made of organic material. After 1 minute of eating, you gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to 1 d6 plus your Proficiency Bonus, and you can expend a Hit Point Die to regain Hit Points equal to the number rolled on the die plus your Constitution modifier. You can’t benefit from eating matter taken from the same creature until you finish a Long Rest.
	***Magic in the Meal***. Your stomach draws power from what you’ve eaten. When you use your Gourmand of Variety trait, provided the creature died no more than 1 hour before, you can also gain an additional benefit depending on the creature’s highest ability score (choose in case of a tie). The benefit lasts for 1 hour, and for the duration, you have Advantage on ability checks and saving throws using that ability. Once you gain a benefit, you can’t gain that benefit again until you finish a Long Rest.
	**Strength**. When you make a damage roll, you can reroll one of the dice and use the new roll.
	**Dexterity**. Your Speed increases by 10 feet.
	**Constitution**. If you have no Temporary Hit Points at the start of your turn, you gain a number of them equal to your Constitution modifier plus your Proficiency Bonus.
	**Intelligence**. You have telepathy with a range of 30 feet times your Proficiency Bonus, and you can cast Mage Hand (the hand is Invisible). Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for it.
	**Wisdom**. You gain Blindsight with a range of 10 feet.
	**Charisma**. The attitude of Hostile creatures improves to Indifferent toward you.
	***Tongue Lash***. As a Bonus Action, you can lash out with your sticky tongue at a surface or object with a range of 15 feet. If the target is a Tiny or Small object, you can grab it with your tongue and pull it to you. If the target is a Medium or larger object or surface, you can pull yourself to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the target.
