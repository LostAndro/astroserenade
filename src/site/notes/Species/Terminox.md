# Terminox
![Terminox.png](/img/user/images/Terminox.png)
Terminox (TER-mi-noks) are a bio-mechanical people reforged by the discordant tone of Progress through the hyper-logical denial of emotion to build structures of control around their pain. Their indomitable bodies are designed to neither cease nor slow in the face of whatever obstacle stands before them.
Typically driven to achieve perfection in the areas of their obsession, terminox are always seeking the next step of optimization. While some uphold an advancement of the self, finding a solitary path or embedding in a small unit, many assimilate into the collective and join the never-ending march of Progress.
Terminox are a species built of fiendish metal with cables of synthetic flesh that cling to their frame like muscle. Their heads appear approximately skeletal, sometimes connected to cords that resemble hair. The parts that make up their construction are typically colored in shades of gray or red.
### Terminox Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 4–7 feet tall) or Small (about 3–4 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Terminox, you have these special traits
	***Ageless***. You don't suffer from or die of old age.
	***Conductive Frame***. You have Resistance to Lightning damage.
	***Darkvision***. You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.
	***Relentless March***. When you drop to 0 Hit Points but aren’t killed outright, you can choose to ignore the effects of the Unconscious condition until you regain any Hit Points. You must still make Death Saving Throws until you regain any Hit Points or become Stable. If you take an action while you are at 0 Hit Points, you suffer a Death Saving Throw failure unless that action stabilizes you or restores you to 1 Hit Point or more. Once you ignore the Unconscious condition with this trait, you can’t do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
	***Strength of Metal***. You have Resistance to Poison damage, and you have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Petrified and Poisoned conditions.
	***Path of Progress***. When you reach character level 3, you must choose the path your discordant nature takes.
	**Adaptive Plating**. Your creature type becomes Fiend. When you take damage, you can take a Reaction to change the damage type of your Conductive Frame trait to the triggering damage type for 1 minute. You can end the change early at any time (no action required). You can use this Reaction a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
	**Collective Protocol**. While you can see at least one ally within 60 feet of yourself, when you or one such ally fails a D20 Test, you can take a Reaction to grant a bonus to the total equal to your Proficiency Bonus, possibly turning the failure into a success. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
