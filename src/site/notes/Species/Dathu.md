#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Dathu
![Dathu.png](/img/user/images/Dathu.png)
Dathu (DAH-thoo) are a ferocious people who have been transformed by the discordant tone of Instinct through their wrath and desire to tear down the perceived source of their pain. Despite this corruption, they remain free and can choose to restrain their aggressive urges or focus their rage on something rather than their impulse to commit mindless acts of destruction.
Many dathu follow pursuits that allow them to give in to their destructive nature, favoring groups and organizations they can link to their personal hivemind. Others surrender to their corruption and join the endless skittering swarms of cacophony.
Dathu possess insectile traits found in the forces of Instinct, with scaly skin and chitinous carapaces, often streaked with amber-colored resin. Their jaws are split into mandibles lined with jagged teeth that drip with corrosive acid.
### Dathu Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 5–7 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Dathu, you have these special traits
	***Ageless***. You don't suffer from or die of old age.
	***Acid Spray***. You know the [[Acid Splash\|Acid Splash]] cantrip. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for it.
	You can modify the spell to cast it as a Bonus Action. When you cast the spell in this way, you can change the area to a 30-foot Line that is 5 feet wide that sprays from you. You can modify this spell a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
	***Corrosive Blood***. You have Resistance to Acid damage.
	***Hive Mind***. You have telepathy with a range in feet equal to 10 times your character level.
	***Wrathful Devour***. As an action, you can consume 1 pound of organic matter and expend and roll a Hit Point Die. You regain a number of Hit Points equal to the number rolled plus your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1 Hit Point).
	***Path of Instinct***. When you reach character level 3, you must choose the path your discordant nature takes.
	**Swarm Survival**. When you finish a Long Rest, you can choose a number of creatures you can see equal to your Proficiency Bonus and form a telepathic hive mind with them that lasts until you next finish a Long Rest. You and the chosen creatures can communicate telepathically with each other out to the range of your Hive Mind. Additionally, when you regain Hit Points from your Consume trait, you can grant those Hit Points to one of the chosen creatures instead if they are within range of your Hive Mind.
	**Unending Hunger**. Your creature type becomes Fiend. When you deal Acid damage to a creature, before the end of your next turn, you can use your Consume trait as a Bonus Action (no additional organic matter required).
