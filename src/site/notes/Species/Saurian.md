# Saurian
![Saurian 1.webp](/img/user/images/Saurian%201.webp)
Saurians (SOR-ee-in) are the oldest species in the Stardust Rhapsody, appearing as humanoid versions of the galaxy’s numerous dinosaurs and similarly prehistoric reptiles. They bear a special connection with the Harmony and can channel its lifegiving energies through that spark.
This connection manifests in an intimate ancestral connection that grants them special insight on how to approach their daily lives.
Across five distinct lineages, saurians comprise a wide range of body types and statures, with each one reminiscent of a broader category of dinosaur. These grant them the traits of water-dwellers, fliers, predators, scavengers, and hardy grazers. They typically have naturalistic hues, but some have vibrantly prismatic scales.
### Saurian Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 5–7 feet tall) or Small (about 3–4 feet tall), chosen when you select this species
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Saurian, you have these special traits
	***Ancestral Memory***. You have proficiency in the Arcana, History, or Religion skill.
	***Harmonious Conduit***. As a Magic action, you touch a creature and roll a number of d6s equal to your Proficiency Bonus. The target regains a number of Hit Points equal to the total rolled. Once you use this trait, you can’t use it again until you finish a Long Rest.
	***Natural Weapon***. You have a natural weapon you can use to make Unarmed Strikes. It deals 1d6 damage of a type appropriate to the weapon instead of the normal damage; Bludgeoning for a slam (club, tail, or headbutt), Piercing for a bite or gore, or Slashing for a claw. Your Saurian Lineage trait determines your natural weapon.
	***Saurian Lineage***. You are part of a lineage that determines some of your physical characteristics and traits. Choose a lineage from the Saurian Lineages table and gain the benefits of that lineage.
