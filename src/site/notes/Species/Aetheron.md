#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Aetheron
![Aetheron 1.png](/img/user/images/Aetheron%201.png)
Aetheron (EE-ther-awn) are a resilient people made of the stuff of space itself, allowing them to manipulate their connection to gravity and resist the effects of [[aether exposure\|aether exposure]]. They typically prefer to live in outer space, often residing in massive space stations or spending their days aboard starships.
Many aetheron believe that they were crafted by [[Khorabos\|Khorabos]] the Architect from a proto form of aether that drifted through the cosmos as a crystalized rock. They often seek orderliness in their lives that matches the tenets of their god, and this sense of order seems to manifest around them innately.
They are relatively short, often stoutly built, and are heavier than their size would suggest. Their skin tones are usually muted with casts of dull gray, blue, or purple, but they often have vibrant streaks of aether hues in their hair.

### Aethheron Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 4–5 feet tall) 
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Aetheron, you have these special traits.
	***Aetheric Lock***. You have Advantage on D20 Tests to avoid or end the Prone condition, or to avoid being moved from your space.
	***Architect’s Blessing***. When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you makes a D20 Test with Advantage or Disadvantage, you can enforce cosmic order and cause the roll to have neither Advantage nor Disadvantage. Once you use this trait, you can’t do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
	***Tireless Core***. You have Advantage on saving throws made to avoid or end the Exhaustion condition.
	Additionally, when you gain Exhaustion, you can reduce the number of levels gained by 1. Once you use this trait to reduce an Exhaustion level, you can’t do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
	***Unyielding Density***. When you take damage, you can take a Reaction to reduce the damage by 1d12 plus your Proficiency Bonus. The first time you deal damage before the end of your next turn, one target takes extra Force damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
	You can take this Reaction a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
