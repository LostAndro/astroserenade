# Laika
![Laika.png](/img/user/images/Laika.png)
Laika (LIE-kuh) are a canine species that resemble bipedal dogs of any breed, with an innate sense of loyalty to their community and anyone they consider a packmate.
While all laika have a powerful bark and bite, most prefer to focus their efforts on cooperation and teamwork, particularly in the areas best suited to the talents of their respective canine lineage. Many of them strive to make a difference in their society, and a good number of historical laika have greatly benefited societies across the galaxy.
Laika come in all shapes, sizes, and physical characteristics that evoke canine breeds. They are covered in fur of varying length and thickness, which typically features naturalistic colors and patterns but can sometimes be vibrant and prismatic in hue.
### Laika Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 5–6 feet tall) or Small (about 3-4 feet tall), chosen when you select this species
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Laika, you have these special traits
	***Bite***. You have sharp teeth you can use to make Unarmed Strikes with the Finesse property. When you deal damage with your bite, you can deal 1d8 Piercing damage plus your Strength modifier, instead of the normal damage of an Unarmed Strike. You can grapple with your bite.
	***Keen Senses***. You have proficiency in the Insight, Perception, or Survival skill.
	***Laika Lineage***. You are part of a lineage that determines some of your traits. Choose a lineage from the Laika Lineages table. You gain the Trait benefit of that lineage immediately, and at character level 3, you gain the Pack Minded benefit.
	***Pack Minded***. When an ally you can see within 30 feet of yourself fails a D20 Test, you can take a Reaction to encourage that ally. The target adds 1d6 to the total, possibly turning the failure into a success. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
	Starting at character level 3, you and the target gain an additional benefit determined by your Laika Lineage.
