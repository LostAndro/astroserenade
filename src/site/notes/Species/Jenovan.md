#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Jenovan
![Jenovan.png](/img/user/images/Jenovan.png)
Jenovans (jeh-NOH-vin) are the descendants of an artificial psionic species created through genetic experimentation to achieve a perfected lifeform. For eons, these bio-engineered creations intermingled with their creators until nothing but the jenovans remained, beings of enhanced physicality and fearsome telekinetic power.
Whatever homeworld they once had was lost to legend, so jenovans became a nomadic people who wander across the galaxy and settle numerous planets in tight-knit communities. They often strive to perfect themselves within and without, pushing their abilities to the limit and beyond. Many seek mightier challenges and opponents to test their skills and train against.
Jenovans have an appearance that betrays their genetically engineered origin, with unnaturally smooth skin in tones of gray, green, and purple. Their bodies are adorned with plates of natural armor as well as crystalline protrusions that focus their telekinesis. All jenovans bear either a prominent tail or a head tendril, though many have both.
### Jenovan Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 5–7 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Jenovan, you have these special traits
	***Bioengineered***. You have proficiency in the Acrobatics, Athletics, or Stealth skill.
	***Psionic Deflection***. When an attack roll hits you and its damage includes Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage, you can take a Reaction to add your Proficiency bonus to your AC, possibly causing it to miss. If you make an attack miss this way, choose a creature you can see within 30 feet of you that isn’t behind Total Cover. The target takes 1d10 Force damage. You can take this Reaction a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
	***Telekinetic***. You know the [[Kinetic Propulsion\|Kinetic Propulsion]] cantrip and the [[Mage Hand\|Mage Hand]] cantrip (the hand is invisible). 
	Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it (choose the ability when you select this species).
	***Final Form***. Starting at character level 3, you can transform as a Bonus Action and gain the benefits below. The transformation lasts for 1 minute or until you end it (no action required). Once you transform, you can’t do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
	**Energy Reversal**. You can use your Psionic Deflection trait on attacks regardless of damage type.
	**Genetic Perfection**. You gain Expertise in one of your skill proficiencies.
	**Kinetic Levitation**. While you aren’t wearing Medium or Heavy armor, you have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed and can hover.
