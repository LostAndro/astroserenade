#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Marsoom
![Marsoom.png](/img/user/images/Marsoom.png)
Marsoom (mar-SOOM) are a bovine species with especially alien biology that grants them psionic prescience and highly tuned danger sense. They have adapted to the strange red planet they call home, making their physical prowess no less formidable.
Most marsoom believe that strength and safety come through unity, and they are typically welcoming to people of all creeds and origins. While they normally engage in diplomatic pursuits, marsoom are prepared to fight to defend what they value most.
They appear as sturdy bipedal cattle but have large, dark, oblong eyes with a third on their forehead. A pair of antennae sprouts from between their horns, their tails are split into two bushy ends, and their skin, hair, and tufts of fur come in a wide variety of vivid, unnatural colors.
### Marsoom Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 5–8 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Marsoom, you have these special traits
	***Cratering Jump***. As a Bonus Action, you can unleash your inner strength to jump up to your Speed to an unoccupied space. When you land, you can unleash a shockwave in a 10- foot Emanation originating from you. Each creature in that area must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus your Constitution modifier and Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, a creature takes 1d10 Thunder damage and is pushed or pulled (your choice) up to 10 feet toward or away from you. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
	***Danger Sense***. Your antennae tingle when danger is imminent. You have proficiency in the Perception skill. In addition, you can’t be surprised.
	***Herd Instinct***. Once during your turn, when you hit a creature with an attack roll and deal damage, if you or an ally you can see took damage from that creature since the start of your last turn, the target takes an extra 1d6 Force damage.
	***Mighty Horns***. You have a pair of horns, which you can use to make Unarmed Strikes. When you hit with an Unarmed Strike using your horns and deal damage with them, you can deal 1d8 Piercing damage plus your Strength modifier instead of the normal damage for an Unarmed Strike.
	***Split-Second Warning***. When you are hit by an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to force the attacker to reroll the d20 and use the lower roll. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
