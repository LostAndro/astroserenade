# Gorgonite
![Gorgonite.png](/img/user/images/Gorgonite.png)
Gorgonites (GOR-guh-nite) are a species of serpentinefeatured humanoids that emerged from toxic jungles of beautiful but highly venomous flora and fauna. This has made them highly adapted to surviving poisonous environments and all of the threats within, in no small part to their powerful magical gaze.
Many of them have a deep appreciation for the balance of beauty and danger, accepting them as equal and necessary halves to a life truly lived. This can lead them to seek out death-defying opportunities across the galaxy.
Gorgonites have a skin of soft scales that range from earthy to vivid, with all manner of patterning. Instead of hair, gorgonites possess tendrils atop their head that resemble snake tails, which can be shaped into a variety of styles.
### Gorgonite Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 5–6 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Gorgonite, you have these special traits
	***Darkvision***. You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.
	***Gorgon’s Gaze***. As a Bonus Action, you can fix your gaze on a creature you can see within 30 feet of yourself. The target is subjected to the effects of one of the options below (choose when you use the trait). The save DC is 8 plus your Proficiency Bonus and your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma modifier (choose the ability when you select this species).
	You can use this Bonus Action a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
	**Beam**. Eroding rays fire from your eyes. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 1d6 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. This damage increases by 1d6 when you reach character levels 5 (2d6), 11 (3d6), and 17 (4d6).
	**Hypnotic**. Your eyes become captivating pools. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the Charmed condition for 1 minute. If you or your allies damage the target or force it to make a saving throw, the condition ends on it, and it is immune to your gaze for 24 hours.
	**Petrifying**. Your eyes flare with dull color. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have the Restrained condition for 1 minute as it partially turns to stone. A creature with Immunity to the Petrified condition succeeds automatically. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
	***Keen Senses***. You have proficiency in the Insight, Perception, or Survival skill.
	***Toxin Resistant***. You have Resistance to Poison damage, and you have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.
