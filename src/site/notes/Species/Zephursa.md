#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Zephursa
![Zephursa.png](/img/user/images/Zephursa.png)
Zephursa (zeh-FUR-suh) are a small, ursine species that hail from floating cities atop a sea of clouds. They bear a special connection to the skies, allowing them to call the wind and even puff up in a cloudy form. They value the power of friendship, which they can genuinely manifest and harness to overcome challenges.
With a spark of ingenuity and a clever knack for invention, many Zephursa are drawn to tinkering with gizmos and developing new ways of approaching daily tasks. Many zephursa are most comfortable soaring through the skies of worlds across the galaxy, but there are those who stay more grounded and find their joy in the community that surrounds them.
Zephursa are little humanoid bears with colorful fur, often with pastel hues although more natural colors do appear. They have swirling cloud patterns somewhere on their coat that sometimes grow into bushier tufts of fluff. The clouds that encircle them when they puff up usually take on the prismatic color of their fur.
### Zephursa Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Small (about 3–4 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Zephursa, you have these special traits
	***Children of the Skies***. You know the [[Befriend\|Befriend]] cantrip. Additionally, starting at character level 3, you always have the [[Fog Cloud\|Fog Cloud]] spell prepared, and starting at character level 5, you always have the [[Gust of Wind\|Gust of Wind]] spell prepared. You can cast each one once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast them in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast the spells using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
	Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait (choose the ability when you select this species).
	***Cloud Puff***. As a Bonus Action, you can puff up with swirling cloud stuff and gain a Fly Speed equal to half your Speed for 1 minute.
	When you are hit by an attack roll during this time, you can take a Reaction to subtract 1d4 from the total, possibly causing it to miss.
	Once you puff up with this trait, you can’t use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
	***Gentle Descent***. While you don’t have the Incapacitated condition, when you fall, your rate of descent is 60 feet per round, and you take no damage from falling.
	***Soothing Stare***. As a Magic action, you target a creature you can see within 30 feet of you and end one of the following conditions affecting it: Charmed, Frightened, or Stunned.
	You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
