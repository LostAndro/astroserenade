#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Motlien
![Motlien.png](/img/user/images/Motlien.png)
Motliens (MOT-lee-in) are an absurd people twisted by the discordant tone of Thrill through their indulgence in sensory overload to drown out their pain. They relish in antics and folly that spark maximum excitement, pleasure, and pain, all of which fuel their cartoonish and reality-warping magic.
Driven to be thrill seekers at heart, many Motliens crave the most dangerous or bombastic environments they can find. Others turn to sharing their laughter, fighting their darker impulses lest they join the hellish circus of Thrill.
Motliens resemble fools, jesters, and clowns, with rubbery, colorful skin marked with strange psychedelic or motley patterns. Their heads sprout fleshy tendrils that resemble a cap and bells, and they often have honkable noses, razorsharp teeth, and long, slithery tongues.
### Motlien Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 4–7 feet tall) or Small (about 3–4 feet tall), chosen when you select this species
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Motlien, you have these special traits
	***Ageless***. You don't suffer from or die of old age.
	***Motley Tricks***. You know the[[ Minor Illusion\| Minor Illusion]] and [[Vicious Mockery\|Vicious Mockery]] cantrips.
	Additionally, starting at character level 3, you always have the [[Hideous Laughter\|Hideous Laughter]] spell prepared, and starting at character level 5, you always have the [[Suggestion\|Suggestion]] spell prepared. You can cast each of these spells once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast them in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast them using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
	Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait (choose the ability when you select this species).
	***Toon Force***. You can take a Reaction when you gain one of the following conditions to end it before it takes effect: Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned, Prone, or Stunned. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
	***Twisted Mind***. You have Resistance to Psychic damage.
	***Path of Thrill***. When you reach character level 3, you must choose the path your discordant nature takes.
	**Honkweave**. Your creature type becomes Fiend. As a Bonus Action, you can take on a wickedly cartoonish form for 1 minute. Once during each of your turns during that time, you can expend movmeent to teleport an equall distance to an unoccupied space you can see. Once you transform using this trait, you can’t do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
	**Laugh it Off**. You can use your Toon Force trait on an ally you can see within 30 feet of yourself when it gains one of the listed conditions. Additionally, when you end a condition using that trait, a creature you can see within 30 feet of yourself must succeed on a Charisma saving throw (DC 8 plus your Proficiency Bonus and the ability modifier you chose for Motley Tricks) or gain the condition you ended.
