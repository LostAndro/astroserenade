#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Glowblin
![Glowblin 1.png](/img/user/images/Glowblin%201.png)
Glowblins (GLOH-blin) are small folk who feel big and intense emotions with an inner neon radiance. This emotional glow colors their physical appearance and shines with vibrant light.
Many glowblins tap into this magic to entertain and influence, shaping light and sound to evoke powerful feelings. They're drawn to fun, color, music, and all things maximalist.
Glowblins have colorful skin, hair, and luminous eyes that change to match their affinity for emotion and selfexpression, taking on a wide spectrum of hues and shades. They also have large, expressive ears, sharp teeth, and prominent, often pointed noses.
### Glowblin Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Small (about 3–4 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Glowblin, you have these special traits
	***Dazzle***. You know the [[Dancing Lights\|Dancing Lights]] cantrip. Additionally, starting at character level 3, you always have [[Color Spray\|Color Spray]] prepared, and starting at character level 5, you always have [[Calm Emotions\|Calm Emotions]] prepared. You can cast each spell once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast them using spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
	Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells (choose the ability when you select this species).
	***Emotional Spectrum***. Choose one of the options below; when you finish a Long Rest, you can choose a new option. Your chosen option can change your skin, hair, or eye color.
	As a Bonus Action, for the next minute, you shed Bright Light in a 10-foot Emanation originating from you and Dim Light for an additional 10 feet. You infuse the light with your chosen color and emotion to provide its benefit, and you can change your chosen color as part of the Bonus Action.
	You can use this Bonus Action a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
	**Blue or Teal**. You and your allies within the light have Resistance to Psychic damage and have Advantage on saving throws made to avoid or end the Charmed or Frightened condition.
	**Green**. When you or your allies in the light regain Hit Points, add your Proficiency Bonus to the number regained.
	**Orange**. Your Speed increases by 10 feet. When an ally enters the light for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, its Speed increases by 10 feet until the end of its next turn.
	**Pink or Purple**. Ability checks made to use the Influence action on an Indifferent creature in the light have Advantage.
	**Yellow**. Double the range of your shed light. You and your allies in the light have Advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) and Wisdom (Perception) checks. The first time an affected creature misses with an attack roll during this use of the light, the creature can add 1d4 to the total, possibly turning the miss into a hit.
	***Passionate Presence***. You have proficiency in the Deception, Performance, or Persuasion skill.
