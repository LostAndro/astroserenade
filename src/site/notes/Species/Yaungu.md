# Yaungu
![Yaungu.png](/img/user/images/Yaungu.png)
Yaungu (YAWN-goo) are a hearty people born of the cold, bearing a deep, supernatural connection to ice and snow. Having descended from the giants of the frozen mountains and tundras of their frigid homeworld, they have adapted to thrive and build remarkable civilizations in the most inhospitable of environments.
Many yaungu have a deep appreciation for the sharing of and passing down of knowledge, whether it be through story, song, or written text. Some even seek out well-hidden truths or heavily guarded secrets so that the learnings can be shared by the many to lead to a brighter future.
Yaungu tend to be tall and powerfully built, with long arms and prominent claws for climbing. Horns grow from the sides of their head, which many of them decorate with carvings or other adornments. Their shaggy, insulating fur is typically white or some shade of blue, but some yaungu sport pelts of brown, gray, or black.
### Yaungu Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 6–8 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Yaungu, you have these special traits
	***Blizzard Cunning***. You have Blindsight with a range of 5 feet. As a Bonus Action, you can increase the range to 20 feet for 10 minutes. Once you use this Bonus Action, you can’t use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
	***Frigid Resilience***. You have Resistance to Cold damage.
	***Frozen Soul***. As a Magic action, you create ice, freeze water, or shape existing ice that fills up to a 5-foot Cube. You can decide the shape the ice takes, though the forms are simple or crude unless you have proficiency in appropriate tools to create intricate or complex shapes. Unless the environment is freezing cold, the ice lasts for 1 minute and then melts or vanishes. Once you use this trait, you can’t use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
	***Long Arms***. You have a Climb Speed equal to your Speed, and your reach with your arms increases by 5 feet.
	***Powerful Build***. You have Advantage on any D20 Test you make to end the Grappled condition. You also count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity.
	***Torpid Chill***. When you hit a target with an attack roll and deal damage, you can deal an extra 1d6 Cold damage. Unless the target has Resistance or Immunity to Cold damage, it also has Disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws until the end of your next turn. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
