# Bitling
![Bitling 1.png](/img/user/images/Bitling%201.png)
Bitlings (BIT-ling) are a widely varied species that hail from the video game realm of Arcadia. The imaginative creativity and bombastic energy of the arcade plane infuse them with whimsical powers that change based on their inherent archetype—strong, swift, or soaring.
Bitlings often travel through Arcadia and the galaxy beyond seeking shiny collectibles that power their magic. Many set out on grand quests seeking fun adventures, sidekicks to befriend, and loot to add to their collection.
Unified by bright colors and exaggerated proportions, a bitlings’s appearance is only limited by one’s imagination. Their most common form is a bipedal animal of any variety, but there are those who resemble toy-like humanoids, walking inanimate objects, or even bizarre oblong shapes.
### Bitling Traits
**Creature Type**: Fey 
**Size**: Medium (about 4 –7 feet tall) or Small (about 3 –4 feet tall), chosen when you select this species
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Bitling, you have these special traits
	***Collectible Drop***. When you finish a Long Rest, you gain a token that looks like a digital collectible (coin, ring, gem, etc.). When you fail a D20 Test, you can expend one token to add 1 d4 to the total, potentially causing it to succeed instead.
	When an enemy within 30 feet of you is reduced to 0 Hit Points, a token appears in its space and remains for 1 minute or until you collect it. You can have a maximum number of tokens equal to your Proficiency Bonus at one time.
	***Arcade Archetype***. Your Arcadian nature grants you certain benefits. Choose Soaring, Strong, or Swift:
	**Soaring**. You have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed while you're not wearing Medium or Heavy armor. As a Magic action, you can expend a token and touch an ally to give it a Fly Speed equal to your Fly Speed for 1 minute.
	**Strong**. You have a Climb Speed equal to your Speed, and you have Advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws. Additionally, once per turn when you hit a target with an attack roll, you can expend a token to push the target up to 20 feet straight away. If the target is an object, it takes double damage from your attack.
	**Swift**. Your Speed increases by 5 feet. As a Bonus Action, you can expend a token to take the Dash action. When you do, until the start of your next turn, your Speed is doubled and your movement doesn’t provoke Opportunity Attacks.
	***Checkpoint***. As a Magic action, you can expend a token to create a shimmering checkpoint on a surface you touch. The checkpoint lasts for 24 hours or until you use this action again. While you're on the same plane of existence, as a Magic action, you can teleport to the point, appearing in its space or the closest unoccupied space. When you do, you can expend a token to bring a willing creature you touch with you.
	***Respawn***. When you drop to 0 Hit Points and don’t die outright, you can expend a token you’re carrying to drop to 1 Hit Point instead. Once you use this trait, you can’t use it again until you finish a Long Rest.
