# Pakku
![Pakku.png](/img/user/images/Pakku.png)
Pakku (PAH-koo) are a species of sapient plants with the combined elements of cacti and carnivorous flora. To survive their deadly, venomous homeworld, they have developed a variety of adaptations to overcome both predators and prey
Favoring the more lawless corners of the galaxy, pakku usually seek their fortune through grit and resolve. Many of them have a strong interest in the crafting and handling of weapons, especially firearms, striving to master the art of the duel with their implement of choice.
Widely varied in shape and size, pakku appear as bipedal plants with spiny cactus bodies that are usually a shade of green. Their heads are typically topped with hair-like vines or colorful, exotic flowers with extra-wide, toothy mouths that resemble carnivorous plants.
### Pakku Traits
**Creature Type**: Plant 
**Size**: Medium (about 4–7 feet tall) or Small (about 3–4 feet tall), chosen when you select this species
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Pakku, you have these special traits
	***Cactus Spines***. Your body is covered in fine, sharp spines you can use to make Unarmed Strikes or use as a Thrown weapon with a normal range of 20 feet and a long range of 60 feet. When you hit with your spines and deal damage with them, you can deal 1d6 Piercing damage plus your Strength modifier instead of the normal damage for an Unarmed Strike. Your spines have the Finesse property.
	***Dissolving Enzymes***. When you deal damage with an Unarmed Strike, a weapon with the Thrown property, or your Spiney Defense trait, you can deal an extra 1d6 Acid or Poison damage. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
	***Fibrous Body***. You have Resistance to Acid and Poison damage.
	***Photosynthesis***. When you finish a Short Rest in Bright Light and expend Hit Point Dice to regain Hit Points, you can roll each expended die twice and use the higher roll. After using this trait, you don’t gain Exhaustion levels from malnutrition for 24 hours.
	***Prickly Skin***. When a creature grapples you, it takes 1d6 Piercing damage.
