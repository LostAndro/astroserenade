# Wrothian
![Wrothian.png](/img/user/images/Wrothian.png)
Most consider wrothians to be avowedly evil, a scourge of The Solar Federation; but by some providence, you were born different. Like all wrothian takers, you were created imperfect, using a fraction of the biomatter allotted to the unholy primarchs. These primarchs rule over your kind, psionically commanding you to go to the small, pale worlds below and harvest their people.
Perhaps you were exiled from your ship for demonstrating some act of mercy, or perhaps you escaped to free yourself from the oppressive primarch regime. Regardless, you are now a Rogue, an enemy of all wrothians and feared by all others.
In abandoning your life with the wroth, you have also forsaken your immortality. Without the sustenance of psionic energy found on wrothian ships, your body will deteriorate and perish in less than a decade. Perhaps this death will be a mercy, or perhaps you will seek some darker alternative to death’s oblivion. Your life’s objective is your own, however, no longer dictated by the primarchs.
### Wrothian Traits
**Creature Type**: Aberration 
**Size**: Medium (about 5-7 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Wrothian, you have these special traits
	***Darkvision***. You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.
	***Psionic Blast***. When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of your attacks with a burst of psionic energy against a creature you can see within 60 feet of you. The target must make an Intelligence saving throw (DC 8 plus your Constitution modifier and Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, the creature takes 1d10 Psychic damage, or half as much damage on a successful save. This damage increases by 1d10 when you reach character levels 5 (2d10), 11 (3d10), and 17 (4d10).
	You can use this blast a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
	***Psychic Resistance***. You have Resistance to Psychic damage.
	***Razor Claws***. You can use your teeth and claws to make Unarmed Strikes. You can use your Dexterity modifier instead of your Strength modifier for the attack and damage rolls of your Unarmed Strikes. When you deal damage with an Unarmed Strike, you can deal Slashing damage equal to 1d6 plus your Strength or Dexterity modifier instead of the strike’s normal damage.
	***Telepathy***. You have telepathy with a range of 60 feet.
