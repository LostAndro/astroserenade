#### [[Species\|Species]]
# Flambego
![Flambego.png](/img/user/images/Flambego.png)
Flambego (flahm-BAY-goh) are a colorful avian species that hail from idyllic tropical islands where life is leisurely. Bearing a mystical bond with the revelry god Xavrius the Celebrant, they are an easygoing people who prioritize relaxation and fun as major virtues.
Empowered by the magic of the party, most flambego seek to spread the carefree joy within them to people across the galaxy. They are often drawn to vacation destinations, but they won’t shy away from danger, especially when it threatens their chill vibes.
Flambego possess the traits of all manner of tropical birds, sporting vibrant, colorful plumage and wings that line each of their arms. Their proportions can vary, and while many have long legs, necks, and beaks, others are compact and round.
### Flambego Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 4–6 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Flambego, you have these special traits
	***Celebrant’s Revelry***. You know the [[Befriend\|Befriend]] cantrip. Additionally, starting at character level 3 you always have the [[Heroism\|Heroism]] spell prepared, and when you reach character level 5, you always have the [[Lesser Restoration\|Lesser Restoration]] spell prepared. You can cast each spell once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast them in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast the spells using any spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
	Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for the spells you cast with this trait (choose when you select this species).
	***Chill Under Pressure***. You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed or Frightened condition. 
	***Festive Flourish***. Your jubilant nature manifests in flamboyant ways. When you finish a Long Rest, choose one of the following manifestations, which lasts until you finish your next Long Rest:
	**Good Vibes**. Choose an ability score. You and your allies within 10 feet of you gain a bonus to saving throws that use that ability. The bonus is equal to the ability modifier you chose for Celebrant’s Revelry.
	**Relaxing Revel**. You can lead up to eight creatures in lively song, dance, or other revelry for 1 minute. At the end of the revel, each creature gains the benefit of a Short Rest, regains one expended Hit Point Die, and its Exhaustion level, if any, decreases by 1. A creature can’t benefit from this manifestation again until it finishes a Long Rest.
	**Carefree Flight**. Your Fly Speed becomes magical, and it no longer requires you to use your Arm Wings with free hands, and you can hover.
	***Tropical Skies***. You have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. If you’re wearing Medium or Heavy armor, or holding anything in your hands, you can’t use this Fly Speed.
