# Oozoid
![Oozoid.png](/img/user/images/Oozoid.png)
Oozoids (OO-zoid) are a pliable species of living slime that congealed from the remnants of the first primordial ooze that lingered from the creation of the cosmos. Despite their malleability, they have a brain, eyes, and nervous system that form them into a bipedal shape within a translucent membrane.
While they hail from the deep seas of ooze on their homeworld, oozoids have since spread across the galaxy, and some choose to don special pressurized suits to more easily hold their form in any environment. Many favor selfexpression across various media, including art and the creation of oblong “blobjects.”
Bright and highly saturated colors of slime typically comprise the wide variety of shapes and sizes of oozoids. Some favor a singular color, while others are multi-toned with strong gradients. Many choose to morph their heads to evoke hair-like shapes.
### Oozoid Traits
**Creature Type**: Ooze 
**Size**: Medium (about 5–7 feet tall) or Small (about 3–4 feet tall), chosen when you select this species
**Speed**: 30 feet

As an Oozoid, you have these special traits
	***Acid Resistance***. You have Resistance to Acid damage.
	***Amorphous***. You have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed, and you can move through a space as narrow as 1 foot without expending extra movement to do so.
	***Elastic Lunge***. You add 10 feet to your maximum Long Jump distance and 5 feet to your maximum High Jump distance. You have Resistance to damage you take from falling.
	***Malleable Reach***. Your reach with Melee weapons, Unarmed Strikes, and your hands to manipulate objects increases by 5 feet. Additionally, when you hit with an Unarmed Strike and deal damage with it, you can deal Acid damage equal to 1d6 plus your Strength modifier, instead of the normal damage of an Unarmed Strike.
	***Reconstitution***. As a Magic action, you expend and roll one Hit Point Die. You regain a number of Hit Points equal to the number rolled plus your Constitution modifier.
	Alternatively, when you are reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed outright, you can immediately use this trait (no action required), and you can’t activate it this way again until you finish a Long Rest.
	***Tremorsense***. You have Tremorsense with a range of 60 feet.
