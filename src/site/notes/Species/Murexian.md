# Murexian
![Murexian.png](/img/user/images/Murexian.png)
Murexians (mer-EX-ee-in) are an aquatic species of mollusklike humanoids that emerged from the dark depths of an ocean world. Legend holds that they were created by Karkosa, the enigmatic proto-god of change, who imbued them with the ability to rapidly evolve to best survive the treacherous deep.
Evolution is core to all murexians, and they develop additional adaptations throughout their life to best suit them for their home environment and the galaxy beyond. This allows them to be equally comfortable on land as they are in the water. Many murexians have no fear of the future, trusting that they will adapt to whatever comes.
Murexians have mollusk-shell heads with tentacled faces that resemble cephalopods such as cuttlefish, and their rubbery skin can rapidly change in color and pattern to aid in communication. As they evolve and gain new adaptations, they can take on the physical characteristics of crustaceans and other invertebrates of the sea.
### Murexian Traits
**Creature Type**: Humanoid 
**Size**: Medium (about 5–6 feet tall)
**Speed**: 30 feet

As a Murexian, you have these special traits
	***Amphibious***. You can breathe air and water, and you have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.
	***Cold Resistance***. You have Resistance to Cold damage.
	***Darkvision***. You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet.
	***Evolutionary Adaptation***. You have evolved a gift from Karkosa. Choose one of the following options.
	**Chromatic**. You have proficiency in the Deception or Persuasion skill, and you learn the [[Message\|Message]] cantrip. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it.
	**Electrosensory**. You have proficiency in the Insight or Perception skill. You gain Blindsight with a range of 10 feet, or increase any existing Blindsight's range by 10 feet.
	**Traversal**. You have proficiency in the Athletics or Stealth skill, and you gain your choice of a Burrow Speed or a Climb Speed equal to your Speed.
	***Touch of Karkosa***. When you reach character level 3, you evolve a defensive physical trait of your choice from the options below. You can use the chosen trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
	**Camouflage**. As a Bonus Action, you can give yourself the Invisible condition until the start of your next turn. The condition ends early if you make an attack roll, cast a spell, or force a creature to make a saving throw.
	**Carapace**. When you’re hit by an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to force the attacker to reroll the d20 and use the lower roll.
	**Spines**. When a creature within 30 feet of yourself hits you with an attack roll, you can take a Reaction to deal 1d6 Piercing damage plus your Proficiency Bonus to the attacker, and the attacker has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.
