#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Frozen Origin
Source: Neon Odyssey: Outrunner's Handbook
*Origin Feat (Prerequisite: Frigid Homeworld)*

You have absorbed the essence of your home’s chilling nature or world soul, gaining the following benefits.

***Cold Resistance.*** You have Resistance to Cold damage.

***Winter Walker.*** You ignore Difficult Terrain caused by snow or ice, and while touching snow or ice you have Tremorsense with a range of 60 feet.
