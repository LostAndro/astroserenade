#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Curious
Source: Steinhardt’s Guide to the Eldritch Hunt Player Pack
*Origin Feat*

You gain the following benefits.

***Learn.*** Whenever you make an ability check using a skill you are not proficient in, you can become proficient in that skill until you finish a Long Rest. Once you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest. 

***Observe.*** Immediately after you roll Initiative, you can choose once creature you can see in the same combat. You learn any of the creatures Resistances
