#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Protected
Source: Vampire: The Masquerade – Bound by Blood
*Origin Feat*

You have someone or something guarding you for as long as you can remember, even if you don't know why. You gain the following benefits.

***Luck Points.*** You have a number of Luck Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus and can spend the points on the benefits below. You regain your expended Luck Points when you finish a Long Rest.

***Reroll.*** Whenever you make a d20 test and roll a 9 or lower on the d20, you can spend 1 luck point to reroll the d20. You must accept the second result.

***Stabalize.*** When you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can spend 1 luck point to drop to 1 Hit Point instead.
