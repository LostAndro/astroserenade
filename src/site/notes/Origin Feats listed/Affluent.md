#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Affluent
Source: Dark Matter Revised
*Origin Feat*

You gain the following benefits.

***Heirloom.*** You gain an Uncommon magic item of the GM’s choice, passed down through generations of your family. A [[Bag of Holding\|Bag of Holding]] or [[Cloak of Protection\|Cloak of Protection]] is recommended.

***Stipend.*** You gain 500 Galactic Notes and gain another 500 Galactic Notes whenever you gain a character level thereafter.
