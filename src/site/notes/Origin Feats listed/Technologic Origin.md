#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Technologic Origin
Source: Neon Odyssey: Outrunner's Handbook
*Origin Feat (Prerequisite: Technological or Urbanized Homeworld)*

You have absorbed the essence of your home’s technological nature or world soul, gaining the following benefits.

***Lightning Resistance.*** You have Resistance to Lightning damage

***Techgnostic.*** You gain proficiency in the Computers or Technology skill, and you speak SIGNAL.
