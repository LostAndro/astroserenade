#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Arcane Overload
Source: Arcana Unleashed
*Origin Feat*

You gain the following benefits.

***Cantrip.*** You learn the [[Firebolt\|Firebolt]] cantrip. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (choose when you select this feat).

***Power Surge.*** When you cast an Evocation spell and deal damage with it, you can add your Proficiency Bonus to one damage roll of the spell. Once you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
