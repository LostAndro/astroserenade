#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Blazing Origin
Source: Neon Odyssey: Outrunner's Handbook
*Origin Feat (Prerequisite: Scorching Homeworld)*

You have absorbed the essence of your home’s heated nature or world soul, gaining the following benefits.

***Fire Resistance.*** You have Resistance to Fire damage.

***Heat Adapted.*** You need only half the normal amount of water for your size to avoid dehydration. If you would start burning, you can make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a success, you don’t start burning.
