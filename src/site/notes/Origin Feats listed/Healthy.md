#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Healthy
Source: Vampire: The Masquerade – Bound by Blood
*Origin Feat*

You have, for what ever reason, maintained a physically healthy lifestyle. You gain the following benefits.

***Hit Die Use.*** The first time you would spend a Hit Point Die after completing a Short or Long Rest, you gain the benefit of spending the Hit Die but do not spend it.

***Saving Throw Proficiency.*** Choose one ability in which you lack saving throw proficiency. You gain saving throw proficiency with the chosen ability.
