#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Airy Origin
Source: Neon Odyssey: Outrunner's Handbook
*Origin Feat (Prerequisite: Airy or Storming Homeworld)*

You have absorbed the essence of your home’s airy nature or world soul, gaining the following benefits.

***Damage Resistance.*** You have Resistance to falling damage, Lightning damage, or Thunder damage (chosen when you select this feat).

***Lasting Breath.*** You can hold your breath for a number of hours equal to 3 plus your Constitution modifier (minimum of +1).
