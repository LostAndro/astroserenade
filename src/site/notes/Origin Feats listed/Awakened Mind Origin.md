#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Awakened Mind Origin
Source: Neon Odyssey: Outrunner's Handbook
*Origin Feat (Prerequisite: Eldritch or Psychic Homeworld)*

You have absorbed the essence of your home’s psychically awakened nature or world soul, gaining the following benefits.

***Psychic Resistance.*** You have Resistance to Psychic damage.

***Resilient Mind.*** You have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed or Frightened conditions.
