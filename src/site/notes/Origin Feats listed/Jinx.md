#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Jinx
Source: Dark Matter Revised
*Origin Feat*

You gain the following benefits.

***Mutual Misery.*** When you roll a 1 on the d20 for a D20 Test, choose a creature you can see within 60 feet of you. The target has Disadvantage on the next D20 Test it makes within the next minute.

***Unlucky Number.*** Whenever an enemy rolls a 13 on the d20 for an attack against you or on a saving throw against a spell that you cast, the d20 roll becomes a 1.
