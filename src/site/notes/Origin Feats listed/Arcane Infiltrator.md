#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Arcane Infiltrator
Source: Arcana Unleashed
*Origin Feat*

You gain the following benefits.

***Cantrip.*** You learn the [[Friends\|Friends]] cantrip. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (choose when you select this feat).

***Cunning Diversion.*** You can take the Dodge action as a Bonus Action. You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
