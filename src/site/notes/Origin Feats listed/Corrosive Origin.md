#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Corrosive Origin
Source: Neon Odyssey: Outrunner's Handbook
*Origin Feat (Prerequisite: Acidic, Radioactive, or Toxic Homeworld)*

You have absorbed the essence of your home’s corrosive nature or world soul, gaining the following benefits.

***Damage Resistance.*** You have Resistance to the damage type that corresponds to your homeworld: Acid (Acidic), Necrotic (Radioactive), or Poison (Toxic).

***Toxin Resilience.*** You have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.
