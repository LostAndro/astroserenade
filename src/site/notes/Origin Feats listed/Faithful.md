#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Faithful
Source: Steinhardt’s Guide to the Eldritch Hunt
*Origin Feat*

You gain the following benefits.

***Divine Clarity.*** If you fail a saving throw against the Charmed or Frightened condition, you can choose to succeed instead. Once you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest. 

***Trial of Faith.*** Whenever you roll a 1 on a d20 Test, you gain Heroic Inspiration.
