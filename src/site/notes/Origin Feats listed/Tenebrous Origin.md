#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Tenebrous Origin
Source: Neon Odyssey: Outrunner's Handbook
*Origin Feat (Prerequisite: Deathly, Harmonic, or Shadowy Homeworld)*

You have absorbed the essence of your home’s grim nature or world soul, gaining the following benefits.

***Necrotic Resistance.*** You have Resistance to Necrotic damage.

***Shadow Nature.*** You have Advantage on Death Saving Throws, and you can Hide while in a Lightly Obscured area, even while you are in an enemy’s line of sight.
