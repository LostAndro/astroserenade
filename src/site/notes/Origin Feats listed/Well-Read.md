#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Well-Read
Source: Vampire: The Masquerade – Bound by Blood
*Origin Feat*

You grew up with your nose buried in books. You gain the following benefits.

***Creature Lore.*** When you roll Initiative, you can recall lore about a creature you can see within 30 feet of yourself, you know one Immunity, Resistance, Vulnerability, or Trait of that creature. You decide when you use this ability.

***Font of Useful Knowledge.*** When you roll a 1 on Intelligence (Arcana or History) check, you can reroll the die, and you must use the new roll.

***Rare Knowledge.*** If you succeed on a an Intelligence (Arcana or History) check, you learn additional lore. The GM has the final say on what you learn, if anything. The lore might consist of important details, amusing revelations, or even secret lore that has never been widely known. The more information you already know about the thing, the more precise and detailed the information you receive is. That information is accurate by might be couched in figurative language or poetry, as determined by the GM.
