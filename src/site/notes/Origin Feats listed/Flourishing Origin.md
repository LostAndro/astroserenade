#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Flourishing Origin
Source: Neon Odyssey: Outrunner's Handbook
*Origin Feat (Prerequisite: Harmonic, Idyllic, or Verdant Homeworld)*

You have absorbed the essence of your home’s abundant nature or world soul, gaining the following benefits.

***Regrowth.*** The first time you would expend a Hit Point Die to regain Hit Points, you don’t expend it. Once you use this benefit, you can’t use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

***Sure Step.*** When you take the Dash action, you ignore Difficult Terrain until the end of the turn.
