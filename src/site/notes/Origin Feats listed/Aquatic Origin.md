#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Aquatic Origin
Source: Neon Odyssey: Outrunner's Handbook
*Origin Feat (Prerequisite: Oceanic Homeworld)*

You have absorbed the essence of your home’s aquatic nature or world soul, gaining the following benefits.

***Amphibious.*** You can breathe air and water.

***Swim Speed.*** You have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed. If you already have a Swim Speed, it increases by 10 feet.
