#### [[Origin Feats\|Origin Feats]]
# Grizzled
Source: Steinhardt’s Guide to the Eldritch Hunt
*Origin Feat*

You gain the following benefits.

***Overcome.*** Whenever you make a saving throw against the Frightened condition, you gain a bonus to the saving throw equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

***Survive.*** You require half as much food and water to survive and you have Advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks made to forage for food and water. When ever you become Bloodied you gain Heroic Inspiration.
